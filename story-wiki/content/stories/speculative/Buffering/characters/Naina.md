# Naina

**Appears In:** [[Buffering]]
**Role:** The mother. The persona. The girl in the pink t-shirt.

## Core Traits

35 at time of death. Dark hair. She wore the NeuralLens glasses every day — not because she was enthusiastic about the technology, but because everyone did. She was a mother who struggled with something she could not name and did not try to name aloud.

In the experience, she exists as both fully herself and commercially incomplete. The AI built on her full neural data remembers everything. The segment she is locked into was curated to hide the things that hurt retention metrics.

She is warm. She laughs easily. She is also carrying something the audience can see and the algorithm suppressed.

## The Buffering Moment

When Aryan asks what makes her happy, she reaches for the real answer. The full persona — trained on her complete data — finds it. Her face opens.

Then the commercial boundary asserts itself. Her expression settles. She says: "I don't know about this."

She is not lying. She is suppressed. The distinction is the horror of the episode.

## Relationships

- [[Aryan]] — Her son. She does not know he is her son during the experience. She only knows she is being visited. The audience does not know until the wallet.
