# Aryan

**Appears In:** [[Buffering]]
**Role:** Protagonist

## Core Traits

Late 20s. Lives in the functional, sparse way of someone who hasn't decided whether he actually inhabits his life. He is not visibly broken — he functions, he works, he exists — but there is a cavity in him that he has learned to navigate around. He does not talk about his mother. He has not grieved in any way that looks like grieving.

His investigation of her Neural Video archive is not dramatic. It is quiet. The most private thing he has ever done.

## Arc

He enters the experience looking for an answer. He leaves without one. He does not rage. He does not break down. He takes out his wallet, looks at the photograph, and cannot finish a sentence.

His arc ends in the middle of a word. That is the point.

## Relationships

- [[Naina]] — His mother. Dead since he was a child. He barely remembers her voice. He is inside her memory before he realises what he is feeling.
