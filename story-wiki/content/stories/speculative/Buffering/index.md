# Buffering

**Status:** Outlined — Screenplay Draft Complete
**Format:** TV Episode (Black Mirror pitch, ~40 min)
**Genre:** Speculative Fiction / Sci-Fi Drama / Psychological
**Working Premise:** A grieving son uses a future memory-streaming technology to enter a commercial segment of his dead mother's recorded life — only to find the answer he came for was edited out before anyone could buy it.

---

## Synopsis

In 2031, NeuralStream Inc. launches NeuralLens — glasses that record full neural-fidelity memories. Hidden in the terms of service is consent for NeuralStream to license these memories as commercial experience segments, curated for engagement and stripped of sadness.

A century later, ARYAN enters an untagged Neural Video archive segment of a woman named NAINA. He finds a warm room, two beds, and a girl in a pink t-shirt sitting across from him. They talk. They laugh. He asks her what makes her happy. She reaches — almost gets there — then resets: "I don't know about this."

He leaves without his answer. He takes out his wallet. A photograph. In faded handwriting beneath it: MOTHER.

She was trying to tell him. The algorithm decided grief was bad for retention metrics.
