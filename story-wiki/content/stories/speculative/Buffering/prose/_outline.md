## Arc Notes

The episode is structured as a slow reveal. The audience is held in the double reading from Scene 9 onward — they believe they are watching a boy-girl confrontation. The wallet in Scene 10 recontextualises the entire experience scene in a single image.

The episode does not explain whether the answer was suppressed by the algorithm or whether "I don't know" was genuinely Naina's answer. Both are true. That ambiguity is the wound.

**Running time target:** 38–42 minutes.
