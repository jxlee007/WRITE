## ACT ONE — THE WORLD THEY BUILT

---

### SCENE 1 — EXT. CONFERENCE STAGE — DAY — 2031

*A stage. White. Pristine. The kind of white that costs money.*

*Ten thousand people in the dark beyond it. Phones raised. Expectant.*

*A PRESENTER, mid-40s, walks on. No podium. Just him and the light. He's the kind of person who learned charisma the way other people learn languages — practised until it stopped sounding practised.*

*Behind him on the screen: a pair of glasses. Slim. Frameless. Almost invisible. The kind of thing you'd forget you were wearing.*

**PRESENTER**
They said wearables were dead.

*He lets it sit.*

**PRESENTER (CONT'D)**
They were wrong. Because everything before this... was just hardware.

*He picks up the glasses. Holds them up. Turns them once.*

**PRESENTER (CONT'D)**
NeuralLens doesn't record what you see.

*He puts them on.*

**PRESENTER (CONT'D)**
It records what you *feel.*

*Murmur through the crowd. Lean-forward energy.*

**PRESENTER (CONT'D)**
Your memories. Your experiences. Captured in full neural fidelity. Every sensation. Every emotion. Every moment you lived — preserved. Shareable. Yours.

*ON THE SCREEN BEHIND HIM: warm lifestyle footage.*

*A father at his daughter's first steps — both of them laughing.*
*A couple watching a sunset, shoulders touching.*
*A woman at a kitchen table with her friends, wine, the golden hour.*

*All of them wearing the slim glasses. Nobody notices the glasses.*

**PRESENTER (CONT'D)**
NeuralLens.
*(beat)*
Live the moment. Keep it forever.

*Applause. Big. Rising.*

*CLOSE ON: the fine print beneath the product name on screen.*

*"By purchasing NeuralLens, you agree to NeuralStream Inc.'s Terms of Experience, Data & Persona Licensing Agreement (v14.2)."*

*It is on screen for two seconds.*

*Nobody in the crowd reads it.*

*CUT TO:*

---

### SCENE 2 — INT. NEURASTREAM INC. — SERVER FARM — NIGHT — 2031

*Endless rows of servers. Blue light. The steady, oceanic hum of something vast and patient.*

*CHEN, 40s, stands at a glass observation window. He wears the air of a man who stopped being surprised by his own power sometime in his late thirties. He is watching data flow on the other side of the glass — thousands of coloured threads, moving like a river that knows where it's going.*

*His JUNIOR ENGINEER walks up with a tablet.*

**JUNIOR ENGINEER**
Sir. We're at sixty-three million active users globally. Neural capture rate is running at ninety-four percent uptime across all devices.

**CHEN**
*(not turning)*
What's the persona yield?

**JUNIOR ENGINEER**
Ninety-one percent viable. The remaining nine — fragmented capture, users with neurological variance, early adopters who removed the glasses before we had full calibration —

**CHEN**
Can we build something from the nine?

**JUNIOR ENGINEER**
*(hesitates)*
Partial personas. They'd have gaps. Memory islands. The AI would fill in — but the fill would be noticeable if you knew what to look for.

*Chen turns. Looks at him.*

**CHEN**
Nobody knows what to look for.
*(beat)*
Package them. Label them "Impressionist Experience."
*(already walking away)*
Charge a premium.

*The Junior Engineer watches him go.*

*CUT TO:*

---

### SCENE 3 — INT. NEURASTREAM INC. — EDITING SUITE — DAY — 2032

*A room of screens. Six of them. Each one showing a different person's life.*

*A CONTENT EDITOR, late 20s, sits before them. She's eating a sandwich. This is routine.*

*On her central screen: a woman we will come to know as NAINA, 35. Dark hair. The NeuralLens invisible on her face. She is sitting at a kitchen table, alone. Her posture: the particular quality of stillness that is not peace.*

*The Content Editor watches. Makes a note on her tablet.*

*She moves the timeline marker forward. Skips past the kitchen table.*

*Naina at a birthday party. Laughing. A child next to her — a small boy, maybe five. She's lifting him, swinging him. He's shrieking with delight.*

*The Content Editor marks this. Keeps it.*

*She opens a METRIC DASHBOARD alongside the footage:*

```
Engagement Analysis — NAINA Archive
Sad-coded content:    23% engagement retention
Happy-coded content:  87% engagement retention
Recommendation:       Curate for Premium tier
```

*She works efficiently. Methodical. The bleak kitchen table: cut. A walk alone in rain: cut. A moment where Naina holds the small boy's hand in a hospital waiting room, both of them looking at a door: cut.*

*What remains: a woman who laughs. Who moves in sunlight. Who exists in all the fragments where she looked okay.*

*The Content Editor tags the final package:*

```
NAINA — Experience Collection
47 segments. Licensed for public streaming.
Avg duration: 2 min per segment.
Mood tags: Warm / Intimate / Nostalgic / Playful
APPROVED FOR RELEASE
```

*She closes the file. Opens the next one.*

*She never asked who Naina was.*

*CUT TO:*

## ACT TWO — NAINA

---

### SCENE 4 — INT. NAINA'S APARTMENT — VARIOUS — 2032-2033

*The apartment of someone who manages. Small but cared-for. A child's drawings fixed to the refrigerator with magnets. One good lamp. Plants that are mostly alive.*

*The NeuralLens glasses live on a hook by the front door. She puts them on every morning without thinking, the way you put on shoes.*

*MONTAGE — fragments:*

*— Naina making tea. The glasses on her face. She doesn't think about them.*

*— ARYAN, 5, sitting across from her at the kitchen table. Drawing something. She watches him without him knowing.*

*— Naina at a bus stop. Rain. She tilts her face up into it for a moment. The glasses record this.*

*— Aryan asleep in the backseat of a car. Naina driving, one hand on the wheel. She glances at him in the rearview mirror every few minutes. Not checking — looking. Like she is trying to fix him in her memory in a way that has nothing to do with the glasses.*

*— Naina standing at the kitchen table. Still. Staring at something we can't see, or nothing, or something inside herself.*

*The glasses record this too.*

*END MONTAGE.*

---

### SCENE 5 — INT. NAINA'S APARTMENT — ARYAN'S ROOM — NIGHT — 2033

*Small. Safe. A child's room.*

*Naina sits on the edge of the bed. ARYAN, 6, is under the covers, already half-gone. She's tucking the duvet around him with the careful precision of a parent who does this every night and notices it every time.*

**ARYAN**
*(eyes closing)*
What do you want to dream about?

*Naina smiles. Tucks the corner in.*

**NAINA**
I asked you first.

**ARYAN**
*(already drifting)*
I want to dream about us.

*She goes very still.*

*She watches him. Something moves through her face. Too many things at once, none of them sayable.*

*She puts her hand flat on his chest. Feels him breathe.*

**ARYAN (CONT'D)**
*(almost completely asleep)*
Mama.

**NAINA**
Mm.

**ARYAN**
Are you happy?

*She doesn't answer.*

*He's already asleep.*

*She sits there for a long moment. Then she reaches for the NeuralLens glasses on his bedside table — she moved them there without realising she'd done it — and puts them on.*

*She looks at her sleeping son.*

*The glasses record her face in the dark.*

---

### SCENE 6 — INT. NAINA'S APARTMENT — LIVING ROOM — LATER — NIGHT — 2033

*The apartment is quiet. Aryan's door is closed.*

*Naina sits at the kitchen table. The familiar table. The one we've seen.*

*On the table: her phone. A piece of paper. A pen. A glass of water, half-full.*

*Nothing dramatic. Just: things.*

*She picks up the phone. Opens a message. Begins to type.*

*Stops.*

*Deletes it.*

*She picks up the pen. Looks at the paper. Puts the pen down.*

*She takes off the NeuralLens glasses. Sets them on the table beside the glass.*

*For the first time in this story, she is not being recorded.*

*She looks at her hands.*

*She sits there.*

*The camera holds on the glasses on the table.*

*FADE TO BLACK.*

---

### SCENE 7 — INT. NAINA'S APARTMENT BUILDING — CORRIDOR — EARLY MORNING — 2033

*Grey light under the door at the end of the corridor.*

*A NEIGHBOUR knocks. Once. Twice.*

*Silence.*

*She knocks again, louder.*

*Through the small window in the door, visible on the kitchen table: the NeuralLens glasses.*

*The neighbour takes out her phone. Dials.*

*We cut away.*

*SMASH CUT TO:*

> **"2147.**

## ACT THREE — THE EXPERIENCE

---

### SCENE 8 — INT. ARYAN'S APARTMENT — NIGHT — 2147

*Sparse. Functional. The apartment of someone who hasn't decided if he lives here or just sleeps here.*

*ARYAN, late 20s, sits at a desk. He doesn't look like someone who is about to do something significant. That's the point.*

*He opens a device — thin as a folded sheet of paper. Types.*

```
SEARCH:  "Naina [surname] — Neural Archive — NeuralStream"
```

*A result loads immediately.*

```
NAINA [surname]
47 Experience Segments Available
Avg Rating: ★★★★☆ (2.3 million experiences purchased)
```

*He stares at that number.*

*2.3 million people.*

*He scrolls through the segment list. Each one tagged: Warm. Nostalgic. Playful. Intimate. He scrolls past all of them.*

*He stops on one:*

```
NAINA — Segment 31
Duration: 1 min 58 sec
[UNTAGGED]
```

*He looks at it. Something in his face he doesn't show.*

*He buys it.*

*He reaches into his desk drawer. Takes out a headset — sleek, lightweight. The everyday version of a century-old technology. He puts it on.*

*His finger hovers over the play button.*

*He presses it.*

---

### SCENE 9 — INT. THE EXPERIENCE — CONTINUOUS

*White.*

*Then: a room assembles itself. Not dramatically — just there, suddenly, the way a place is when you open your eyes. Soft, natural light. The light of an ordinary afternoon.*

*Two beds. Simple. The room of someone who managed.*

*ARYAN is sitting on one bed.*

*On the other bed, across the room:*

*NAINA.*

*35. Dark hair. Wearing a PINK T-SHIRT — round neck, something written on the front in pink glitter, too small to read clearly from this distance. Blue cotton pyjamas. She is sitting cross-legged, comfortable. Like she belongs here, because she does.*

*She looks at him.*

*She is completely real. That is the first thing he thinks. That is the first thing the audience thinks.*

*She gives him a small, calm smile.*

**NAINA**
Hi.

**ARYAN**
*(a beat)*
Hi.

*He takes her in. She lets him. There is nothing performative about the way she exists in this room.*

**ARYAN (CONT'D)**
Does it feel strange to you? Being... here?

**NAINA**
*(considers it honestly)*
A little. I think it always does. At first.

**ARYAN**
It feels real. That's what's strange about it. It feels completely real and also... not.

**NAINA**
Like a video you're somehow inside.

**ARYAN**
Exactly. Like a video. But there's a version of you — *in* the video. Talking to me. And maybe in real life you're... not—

*He stops himself. There's something careful in him.*

**NAINA**
Not like this?

*She says it without any weight. Like it's a fact she has made peace with.*

**ARYAN**
Yeah.

*A small silence. She tilts her head slightly.*

**NAINA**
*(simply, almost amused)*
I was made for this. I think.

*He looks at her.*

**ARYAN**
That doesn't bother you?

*She considers it. Actually considers it, the way people consider things that are genuinely interesting to them.*

**NAINA**
Does it bother *you?*

*And then — both of them smile at the same time. At the absurdity of that question. At the absurdity of the whole arrangement. A small, shared laugh — quiet, genuine, private.*

*The audience reads it as: two people. A warmth between them. Something beginning.*

*It is a mother and son laughing together about the fact of her death.*

---

*A beat of comfortable quiet. He looks around the room.*

**ARYAN**
Can I ask you something?

**NAINA**
You can ask.

*He looks back at her.*

**ARYAN**
What makes you happy? What's your... favourite thing.

*She smiles first. Reflexively. Warmly.*

*Then her eyes move. Not to him. Somewhere past the room, or through it, or into herself. She is searching.*

*And we watch it on her face: she finds it. Something real. Something specific. Her lips part slightly. She draws breath.*

*The audience leans forward.*

*Then —*

*Her expression settles.*

*Like a screen that was loading something and then quietly, without error, stops.*

*The warmth remains. But the thing that was coming — doesn't.*

**NAINA**
*(soft)*
I don't know about this.

*Simple. Certain. A door that opens inward with no handle on his side.*

*Aryan stares at her.*

*He felt it. That fraction of a second where she had the answer in her hands.*

**ARYAN**
*(very quietly)*
You do know.

*She meets his eyes.*

**NAINA**
I don't know.

*Not defensive. Not evasive. Not sorry.*

*Just — on the other side of something.*

*He looks at her for a long moment. He opens his mouth.*

*Then — from somewhere outside the room. Distant. Through a gate, or a door, or through time:*

*A VOICE.*

*A woman's voice. Calling something. Ordinary. The sound of a mother's voice calling from somewhere outside.*

*It sounds like her voice.*

*(It is her voice. Another memory. Another time. The audience doesn't know this yet.)*

*Aryan glances toward the sound, distracted.*

*Naina hears it too. Something shifts in her. She sits up slightly. Adjusts herself — a small, practised collecting of herself into the shape of someone who is fine.*

*The way she always did when the outside world called.*

*Aryan looks back at her. He sees what she's done. The rearranging. The composure she put back on like a coat.*

*He reaches out — slowly — and sets his hand on her knee.*

*Gently. Just: stop. You don't have to do that. Not here.*

*She looks down at his hand.*

*She becomes still. Then, for just a moment — almost imperceptibly — something lets go.*

*Then the session timer sounds.*

*A single soft CHIME.*

*ON SCREEN, just visible at the edge of the experience:*

```
SESSION ENDING: 00:10
```

*Aryan looks at her.*

```
00:07
```

**ARYAN**
*(barely above a whisper)*
I just wanted to know why you—

```
00:04
```

*Naina looks at him.*

*He is watching her with an expression he has never let anyone see.*

*She looks back with an expression that is almost recognition.*

*She opens her mouth.*

```
00:01
```

*WHITE OUT.*

## ACT FOUR — THE WALLET

---

### SCENE 10 — INT. ARYAN'S APARTMENT — CONTINUOUS — NIGHT — 2147

*He pulls the headset off.*

*He sets it on the desk.*

*He sits in the chair.*

*Silence.*

*He doesn't move for a long moment. The apartment around him: the same as it was. Unchanged.*

*He looks at the device on the desk. The session summary loading:*

```
Experience complete.
Duration: 1 min 58 sec.
Rate your experience:  ★ ★ ★ ★ ★
Would you like to purchase another segment?
```

*He closes the device.*

*He reaches into his pocket. Takes out a WALLET. Old. Soft leather, worn at the corners in the way things get worn when they are carried every day for many years.*

*He opens it.*

*Behind a small plastic window: a PHOTOGRAPH.*

*A woman. 35. Dark hair.*

*A PINK T-SHIRT. Round neck. Something written on it in glitter.*

*She is smiling at the camera.*

*Beneath the photograph, in small, faded handwriting — written by a child, years ago:*

**MOTHER**

*Aryan looks at the photograph.*

*A tear falls. Just one. He doesn't wipe it.*

*He opens his mouth.*

**ARYAN**
Why did you...

*He stops.*

*He can't finish the sentence.*

*He couldn't get the answer.*

*He can't finish the question.*

*He closes the wallet.*

*HOLD on his face.*

*FADE TO BLACK.*

## PRODUCTION NOTES

### The Double Reading — Direction Note

The experience scene (Scene 9) must be directed to sustain two simultaneous readings without betraying the reveal.

**What the audience believes they are watching:**
A young man and a young woman, meeting inside a shared virtual space. A warmth between them. An almost-romantic tension. A guarded woman who won't quite let him in. His quiet frustration at her "I don't know."

**What it actually is:**
A son sitting with his dead mother. His questions are not curiosity — they are grief. Her warmth is maternal. Her "I don't know" is not distance — it is a suppressed answer to the question he has carried his whole life.

*The director should never tip the second reading. The actors must know it. The audience must not.*

---

### The Buffering Moment — Performance Note

The moment when Naina reaches for the answer and then resets is the episode's technical and emotional core. It must NOT read as a machine glitch. It must read as **a human choosing not to answer** — or *being unable* to answer. The audience should not be certain which.

Naina is not broken in that moment. She is suppressed. There is a difference, and the performance must hold it.

---

### The Voice — Sound Design Note

The voice heard from outside the gate in the experience scene is Naina's voice. It is a fragment of another memory — she is calling for Aryan as a child, from somewhere else in her neural archive, bleeding through the commercial segment's boundary.

The sound should be:
- Barely audible
- Warm, not eerie
- Recognisable only in retrospect, after the wallet reveal

On second viewing, it devastates.

---

### The Wallet Photograph

The photograph must show Naina in the pink t-shirt — the same one she wears in the experience. The audience sees it and understands: he knew which experience to buy. He recognised her. He chose the segment where she wore the shirt from the photograph he has carried his whole life.

He went looking for her in the moment he recognised.

---

### The Final Title Card

The title card is the episode's argument, delivered coldly after the emotional impact has landed. It should not appear until the audience has had five full seconds of black silence after Aryan's face.

---

### Running Time

Target: 38–42 minutes.
The episode should feel like a breath held. No scene should run longer than it needs.
