## Arc Notes

**Episode 1** is the discovery arc. He begins knowing nothing. Each scene gives one new piece of reality. The last scene gives him all of it at once, through her.

**Episode 2** is the cost arc. He learns, grows, reaches the peak — then destroys it in a single failure to stay and listen. The warm core, built over a lifetime of solitude, is consumed in the retreat. The hollow is the consequence.

**The Opening sequence (Todo)** must establish the warm core visually before any contact begins — the audience needs to see what is at stake before it becomes a stake.

**Scene 206** is the structural hinge of the series. The Core Pact threshold is reached here — this scene needs the most development work to incorporate the new Black Hole / warm core mechanics.

**The eastern lean** (established in Scene 002) is never reversed — even after Scene 302. This is the one thing his planet does without his permission. It must be consistent across all scenes from 002 onward.
