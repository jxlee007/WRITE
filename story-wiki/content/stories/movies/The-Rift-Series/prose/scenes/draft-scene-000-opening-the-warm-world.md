# Scene 000 — Opening: The Warm World

**Format:** Silent physical acting. Zero mouth movements. Telepathic VO and dedicated sound design.
**Location:** His inner planet — Orbit, Surface Archipelago, and Deep Crust/Core.
**Character:** He. Whole, self-sufficient, battling daily weather storms to preserve his identity.
**Purpose:** Establish his intact warm core and his ongoing workout battling unpredictable planet weather before any external connection arrives.

---

## 🎬 Subtitles & Reflection Guide Track
> *"Every person is a planet — built from the thoughts they process and the storms they endure. Before anyone came, I was whole. My world was my own workout, my own architecture, my own warmth."*

---

## 👁️ Visual Track (Camera & Micro-Expressions)

1. **EXT. ORBIT — HIGH ANGLE (WIDE):**
   - Camera floats high above his planet. It is not a solid sphere, but a sprawling **archipelago of scattered islands** floating across a deep, dark ocean.
   - In the north: a complex system of towers connected by suspended walkways.
   - In the south: flat sediment islands with organized, private objects arranged by hand.
   - In the far-off secluded corner: a distant, dark, jagged rock formation (**Skull Island**) sits quietly in the haze, unvisited.

2. **SURFACE — WEATHER BATTLES (EXTREME WEATHER):**
   - An unpredictable lightning storm and seismic quake strike the northern towers. Earth crackles; walkways shudder.
   - **He** stands on a high walkway. His eyes are wide, focused, determined. His lips remain firmly closed—no spoken words.
   - He leaps across the swaying walkway, anchoring a loose structural cable to preserve the island's originality. He works relentlessly, battling the tempest, keeping his islands floating.

3. **INT. PLANET CRUST / CORE — DEEP VERTICAL DOWNWARD CUT:**
   - The camera plunges past the surface islands, through the ocean floor, into the crust.
   - Deep beneath all islands: a magnificent, pulsating **Warm Core** glows with amber and golden light. It illuminates the underside of his islands.
   - He descends through a Den, standing at the edge of his inner glow. His face relaxes; a quiet, self-sufficient warmth reflects in his eyes.

---

## 🔊 Sound Design Track

- **Orbital Texture:** Deep, oceanic space hum with subtle high-frequency star static.
- **Weather Storm Foley:** Violent thunderclaps, crackling lightning hitting towers, roaring winds, heavy seismic grinding of island crusts.
- **Warm Core Signature:** A rich, low sub-bass warmth (40Hz harmonic drone) that resonates whenever his core is on screen.

---

## 🧠 Telepathic VO Track

*(Mental Voice — resonant, spatial reverb texture; lips remain stationary)*

> **HE (V.O.):** "The weather here comes without warning. You don't predict it; you fight it. Every island is a thought I built with my own hands. Every storm is a test to keep what's mine floating. I thought this was everything."
