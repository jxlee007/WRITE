# He (Unnamed)

**Appears In:** [[../index.md|The Rift Series]]
**Role:** Protagonist
**Format constraint:** Unnamed. Never referenced by name within the story.

---

## Core Identity

He is a person who built his world carefully, alone, over a long time — constantly battling unpredictable weather storms and quakes to preserve the integrity of his islands. He is satisfied with what he built, without knowing that self-sufficient isolation is its own kind of incompleteness.

He is not broken at the start. He is **whole in the way a person is whole before they discover what they were missing**. His warm core is intact. His islands are dense with processed thought.

He knew about Core Pacts conceptually, but had never witnessed or experienced one — all his past connections were standard surface wormhole rifts.

---

## Planet — Exterior (Orbital View)

**Structure:** An archipelago of scattered islands across a wide, dark ocean.

| Island / Region | Psychological Material |
|---|---|
| Northern island cluster | A system of towers connected by walkways — built to solve the same problem repeatedly, never quite to satisfaction. The work of refinement. |
| Southern plain islands | Flat, compressed sediment — things processed and set down, not discarded. Objects arranged in private logic no one else can fully read. |
| Western edge | An ocean. The oldest part of the planet. Not designed — it arrived. Colour: the specific dark of water that holds memory. |
| Eastern rim | The direction of sensitivity. Where the proximity feeling arrives strongest. Where he stands when he is paying attention to something outside himself. |
| **Skull Island (Far Corner)** | A jagged, dark, imposing island in the furthest secluded corner of his planet. **Represents buried anger, primal possessiveness, and repressed rage.** Inspired by King Kong: Skull Island. Invisible from orbit; only visible upon landing and looking into the far distance. He has never accessed it before. |

**Weather Mechanics (Life's Turbulence):** Constantly battling atmospheric storms, earthquakes, and disasters. His life is an ongoing workout of fighting against unpredictable weather events to keep his constructed islands floating and intact.

**Warm core (pre-story):** Visible from inside as a deep glow beneath all islands. A settled, self-sufficient warmth.

## Planet — Interior (What Is Found Inside)

> **Note:** Interior is not shown in Episodes 1–2. It is seeded visually — she is shown the surface and the exterior, never taken inside. The hollow is established by Episode 2's end but not yet discovered by anyone.

**Pre-pact:** Warm, layered, private. The internal geography is coherent but legible only to him.

**Post-pact break (end of Episode 2):** The warm core is gone. The interior is hollow — a cavity the exact shape and volume of what was there. The exterior of the planet is unchanged. From orbit it still looks full. From inside, the silence is absolute.

## Key Behavioural Patterns

- **Does not draw conclusions before evidence is complete** — methodical, careful, slow to act
- **Does not announce himself loudly** — his presence registers atmospherically, not directly
- **Built his world to take weight** — towers designed for storms; he processes things, he doesn't collapse
- **Reaches only when he is sure** — which means he sometimes reaches too late, and sometimes not at all
- **Pattern:** When he feels something he hasn't felt before and hasn't yet processed — he retreats to his world, to his arrangement of objects, to the towers. He returns to the familiar until the unfamiliar becomes legible. This pattern is his greatest strength and, in Episode 2 Scene 301, his decisive failure.

## Visual / Sound Signature

- **Visual:** Dark atmospheric palette — cool tones, precise architecture, deliberate organisation. No overgrowth. Nothing left where it doesn't belong.
- **Sound:** The warm core tone (sub-bass, felt not heard) present from the start. Fades incrementally after Scene 302. By Scene 304 it is gone. The hollow silence — the specific absence of the tone — becomes its own sonic presence.
- **The lean:** His planet leans east when she is near. This is the one thing his planet does without his permission. It is never reversed — even after the rift closes, the lean remains.
