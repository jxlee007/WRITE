# DJ (Unnamed Friend)

**Appears In:** [[../index.md|The Rift Series]]
**Role:** Supporting Character (Mutual Surface Friend — Wormhole Rift)
**Format constraint:** Unnamed. Referenced visually / sonically as "DJ".

---

## Core Identity

DJ is a close, long-time friend of **[[She.md|She (Girl 1)]]** and hosts a vibrant **Party Island** on his planet where Her wider circle of 2–10+ surface friends gather. 

Crucially, DJ also becomes a genuine **good friend to Protagonist ([[He.md|He]])** as Protagonist learns to navigate social connections. Unlike the intense Core Pact between Protagonist and Girl 1, DJ and Protagonist build a healthy **surface friendship (wormhole rift)**. 

Even after the catastrophic Core Pact collapse between Protagonist and Girl 1 in Episode 2, **DJ's surface friendship with Protagonist remains intact** — proving that surface friendships can survive when deep romantic pacts break.

---

## Planet — Exterior & Atmosphere

- **Party Island & Rift Hub:** A dedicated island designed for gathering, rhythm, and shared soundscapes. Connected to multiple inner worlds via stable surface wormholes.
- **Atmosphere:** Dynamic, welcoming, rhythm-infused. The weather on DJ's planet is communal and uplifting.

---

## Relational Role in Episode 2

1. **Introduction & Friendship:** Girl 1 introduces Protagonist to DJ. DJ welcomes Protagonist, helping him open up to social connections. Protagonist and DJ form a genuine surface friendship.
2. **The Party Gathering (Scene 301):** Girl 1 and Protagonist attend a gathering on DJ's Party Island with Her broader social circle.
3. **The Catalyst Event:** A stranger / friend of a friend at the party interacts closely with Girl 1.
4. **The Jealousy Outburst:** Protagonist encounters romantic jealousy for the first time, unlocking his hidden **Skull Island** (buried rage). His aura turns fearsome, scaring Girl 1. He panics and flees.
5. **Post-Break Standing:** While the Core Pact with Girl 1 is severed in Scene 302, DJ remains a friend to Protagonist via their surface wormhole rift.

---

## Significance to Lore

- **Proves Surface Friendship Resilience:** Demonstrates that surface wormhole friendships do not collapse when a separate Core Pact breaks. DJ remains a social anchor for Protagonist.
- **Warm Core Rebuild Seed:** DJ's ongoing surface friendship is the key mechanism that will eventually allow Protagonist's hollow planet to rebuild its warm core in later story arcs.
