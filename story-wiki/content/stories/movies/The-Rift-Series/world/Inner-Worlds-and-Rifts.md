# Inner Worlds & Rifts — World Reference

> Canonical mechanics reference for [[../index.md|The Rift Series]].

## Presentation & Script Format

### Telepathic Communication & Facial Emotion Cues
- **Telepathic Communication:** Characters communicate mentally through resonance when connected (via rifts or pacts). Their voices are heard on the audio track as a distinct mental voiceover with a spatial reverb/resonant sound texture.
- **Zero Mouth Movements:** Characters **NEVER move their mouths or make lip gestures on screen**. There is zero spoken lip animation or mouth movement when telepathic communication occurs.
- **Acting & Visual Cues:** Emotion is communicated purely through **facial expressions, micro-cues, eye movements, body language, and spatial positioning**.
- **Poetic Voiceover (VO) & Subtitles:** Introspective monologues and inner reflections run in parallel as Subtitles / Reflection Guides, providing deep narrative alignment without breaking the visual silence of the physical characters.

## The Planet (Inner World)

Every person contains a planet — their inner world. The planet is:

- **An archipelago of scattered islands**, not a single unified terrain
- Each island = a specific **thought, mindset, or processed conclusion**
- The collection of all islands = the **personality**
- Islands vary in age, density, size, and ecological character:
  - Old, settled conclusions → large, stable, richly detailed islands
  - Unresolved or recent material → smaller, shifting, sometimes submerged at high tide

### Party Islands & Social Hubs
- Certain social planets (e.g. [[../characters/DJ.md|DJ's planet]]) feature dedicated **Party Islands** designed for gathering, rhythm, shared soundscapes, and welcoming 2–10+ surface friends.
- **Surface Wormhole Persistence:** When a deep Core Pact between two people breaks, **unrelated surface wormhole friendships (e.g. DJ's friendship with Protagonist) do NOT collapse**. They remain intact on the surface layer, providing a vital bridge and social anchor for recovery.

### Hidden Islands & "Versions of a Person"
- A planet can contain far-off, secluded islands located in distant corners of the world, representing buried or dormant emotional states (e.g. intense anger, possessiveness, primal survival instincts).
- Example: **Skull Island** on Protagonist's planet — a jagged, imposing island in the far corner representing repressed anger and possessiveness.
- **Versions of a Person:** Seeing someone's surface planet or visiting their core does not mean you know all "versions" of them. When an unprecedented emotion (like romantic jealousy) triggers a hidden island, the person accesses a dormant "version" of themselves — shifting their atmospheric aura and power dynamics dramatically.

### Weather Mechanics — Life's Unpredictable Turbulence
- **Weather represents life situations:** Storms, earthquakes, quakes, and atmospheric disasters occur unpredictably on a planet. No person can accurately predict when a weather event will hit.
- **The constant workout:** A person cannot prevent weather; they must either fight against it to preserve the originality and integrity of their islands, or work continuously to keep their islands afloat amidst the turbulence.
- **Protagonist's planet:** Constantly battling heavy storms, quakes, and atmospheric turbulence — showcasing his relentless daily struggle to maintain his identity and keep his constructed islands intact.
- **Shared battle & trust:** When a visitor actively helps a planet's owner fight off a weather disaster to preserve their islands, it creates profound gratitude. This shared effort is what prompts a person to open a **Den** and invite the visitor into their interior core.

### Exterior View (Orbital)
- Seen from above, the planet reads as a **gestalt personality portrait**
- Weather, atmospheric colour, the distribution of islands — all legible to someone who knows how to read them
- The planet can look **full, blooming, active** from orbit even when the interior tells a different story

### Interior View (Landed)
- A person can land on their own planet — or be taken inside another's
- Interior exploration reveals structure that orbit cannot: the spaces between islands, underground systems, what holds islands together or keeps them apart
- **The warm core** is only visible from inside — a light that comes from beneath all islands, illuminating the underside of the terrain

### The Hollow Interior
- When a warm core is consumed (see: Core Pact, broken), the planet's interior becomes **hollow** — the light beneath the islands goes out
- From orbit: the planet appears unchanged
- From inside: the space beneath the islands is dark, resonant, empty — a cavity the exact shape of what used to be there
- The person themselves may not initially know their core is gone — it is discovered, not announced

### Planet — Vertical Layers

```
[ Orbit / Space ]              ← visible to anyone; orbital personality read
[ Atmosphere ]                 ← readable to skilled observers; proximity felt here
[ Surface: Islands + Ocean ]   ← open to anyone who lands; public terrain
[ Underwater / Island base ]   ← accessible if already on the planet surface
[ Crust ]                      ← the hidden interior beneath surface and seabed
[ Core ]                       ← deepest layer; only accessible through Dens
```

### Dens — The Access Points
- **Dens** are entrances to the interior — concealed passages found in island terrain or beneath the ocean floor
- A visitor who lands on a planet **cannot locate a den without being shown one** — they do not reveal themselves to uninvited guests
- The planet's owner must **actively guide someone to a den** and choose to open it — this is a deliberate act, not accidental
- Once inside, a guest travels through the crust toward the core
- **Den access is tiered:**

| Access Level | Who Gets It | What They See |
|---|---|---|
| **Orbit** | Anyone | Planetary shape, weather, island distribution — personality gestalt |
| **Surface landing** | Anyone welcomed onto the planet | Islands up close, ocean, open terrain — the presented self |
| **Underwater / island base** | Closer contact — invited deeper | The underside of things; what holds islands up; what is submerged |
| **Crust / Den entrance** | Trusted — explicitly guided | The hidden geography; the transition zone between public and private |
| **Core** | The deepest trust only | The warm core itself — or its absence |

- **Implication for strangers:** A stranger can see the exterior of a person's world and read their public personality. They cannot reach the core unless the person takes them there.
- **Implication for the Core Pact:** The Black Hole forms at the den level — this is where core access would be offered and exchanged in a successful pact. Showing someone your den is already an act of extraordinary trust.
- **Implication for the hollow:** After the warm core is consumed, the person can still live on their planet's surface. The hollow is not visible from outside. It is only discovered when someone is taken through a den — or when the person descends to their own core and finds nothing there.

## The Warm Core

- The **warm core** is the settled, self-sufficient centre of a healthy inner world
- It is not happiness — it is the specific warmth of a person who is complete before external connection arrives
- Visible from inside the planet as a deep glow beneath all islands
- Faintly detectable from orbit by those who know what to look for — a warmth in the atmospheric layer that is not atmospheric
- **Source of the miracle:** A pure heart wish, required to rekindle a broken Core Pact, can only originate from an intact warm core

### Warm Core States
| State | Description |
|---|---|
| **Intact** | Full glow. Planet is whole. Self-sufficient. |
| **Consumed** | Given into the shared Pact space. Interior hollow if Pact breaks. |
| **Rebuilding** | Faint re-emergence. Slow alone. Fast with community. |
| **Restored** | Full glow returns. Planet whole again. |

## Black Holes & The Core Pact

### Wormholes vs. Black Holes (Friendship vs. Core Pact)
- **Normal Friendships (Wormholes / Rifts):** Standard relationships and friendships exist via rifts and wormholes connecting island surfaces. They do NOT require Black Holes or core access.
- **Inner Circle (Black Holes & Core Pacts):** A Black Hole forms only when a relationship transcends normal friendship into an inner-circle bond (*something more than best friend*).
- **Protagonist's Knowledge:** Protagonist knew of Core Pacts conceptually, but had never witnessed or experienced one firsthand, as all his previous connections were standard surface rifts.

### Discovery of the Pact & Her Den
1. **Weather Crisis & Shared Battle:** While exploring Her planet, a severe unpredictable weather storm/disaster hits Her islands. Protagonist actively fights alongside Her against the weather to save and preserve Her islands.
2. **Opening the Den:** Touched and moved by his active support, She trusts him enough to open Her **Den** and take him inside Her planet's crust.
3. **Feeling the Remnant:** Inside Her Den, Protagonist senses a strange, cold presence/cavity — the remnant of a past wound / broken Core Pact from Her past.
4. **Learning the Pact:** He asks Her what this presence is. She explains the Core Pact system to him — how warm cores co-exist in shared space, what it costs, and the risk of hollowness if broken.
5. **Protagonist Proposes the Pact:** Inspired and moved by Her vulnerability, Protagonist suggests: *"If this is how it works, let us make a Core Pact together."*

### The Core Pact Process
When both parties reach the inner circle threshold — when both planets contain a Black Hole oriented toward the other — a **Core Pact** is initiated.

**The Pact process:**
1. Both parties feel the gravitational pull of their respective Black Holes at the Den level.
2. Both offer their warm cores into a **shared trusted space** anchored between the two Black Holes.
3. In a successful pact: warm cores co-exist in the shared space, radiating warmth to both worlds.
4. The shared space is **visible as a luminous zone between their planets** — the most stable, warm, constant light in either world's vicinity.

### The Untold Truth of the Core Pact (Mutual Power & Distortion)
- **The Double-Edged Access:** Establishing a Core Pact carries a profound, untold truth: **by opening your core, you give your partner the power to directly influence your planet.**
- **With Pure Intentions:** It allows partners to heal each other, quiet weather storms, share emotional energy, and co-create stability.
- **With Unstable Intentions or Emotional Outbursts:** It grants terrifying influence. If a partner experiences chaotic emotions (or bad intentions), they can manipulate, confuse, or distort the other's reality—using the partner's own islands and personality against them.
- **Why Protagonist Panics (Scene 301–302):** When Protagonist accesses his hidden **Skull Island** (uncontrolled jealousy/anger) during the incident at DJ's planet, he realizes the terrifying power his dark version could unleash through the Core Pact. Horrified by his own intimidating presence and terrified of warping Her world, he panics and severs the Pact connection.

### Pact Outcomes

| Outcome | Cause | Consequence |
|---|---|---|
| **Successful Pact** | Both fully offer; neither withdraws | Warm cores co-exist in shared space. Both planets feel the other's warmth constantly. The most stable connection possible |
| **One-sided withdrawal** | One party backs out after initiating | Shared space collapses. The abandoning party's warm core is severed — returns to them consumed, spent. Their interior goes hollow. The other party's core is returned intact but damaged by the collapse |
| **Mutual betrayal** | Both parties withdraw | Mutual hollow. Mutual damage |
| **Rekindling** | Miracle — requires pure heart wish from an intact warm core | Nearly impossible after a break. Considered miraculous. Not impossible — miracles exist in this universe, but they require what the break usually destroys |

## Rifts

### Formation
- A **Rift** forms when two inner worlds come close enough in shared space that their atmospheric signatures interact
- The friction of proximity tears a seam between their spaces
- Rifts can form **unconsciously** (instinctive resonance) or **consciously** (learned skill application)

### Rift States
| Type | Cause | Duration |
|---|---|---|
| **Temporary** | Surface resonance only | Flickers, then closes |
| **Permanent** | Deep resonance — fundamental mutual recognition | Stabilises, becomes navigable |
| **Deliberately closed** | Inverted skill — one party withdraws resonance until seam closes | Permanent from closing side |
| **Collapsed** | Resonance began, one party withdrew before completion | Leaves a scar in the spatial fabric — visible as a faint discolouration where the seam almost was |

### What a Rift Looks Like

**From outside:**
- A luminous tear in personal space — textured like a seam, not a wound, not clean-edged
- The interior shows fragments of the other person's world: their colours, their weather, their island architecture
- Each rift has its own warmth signature — readable with experience

**From inside (travelling through):**
- Disorienting, intimate, vertiginous
- You arrive in someone else's constructed reality and experience it from inside their perception

### The Word "Rift"
Deliberate double meaning:
- **Rift as connection** — the wormhole that links two worlds
- **Rift as break** — what happens when a wormhole is closed, collapses, or tears from the wrong angle

## The Proximity Skill

### What It Is
The **ability to sense that another inner world is nearby** — close enough that a rift between them is possible.

### How It Feels
- Not dramatic — atmospheric. A change in the quality of the space around a planet.
- Directional: the feeling has a source. The planet itself **leans** toward it over time (slowly, like a plant toward a window — without deciding to, just following the warmth)
- Distinct from rift detection: proximity sense is pre-rift, the feeling of *possibility*. A rift is the result.

### Skill Stages
| Stage | Action | What Happens |
|---|---|---|
| **Passive sensing** | No effort — just noticing | Feeling arrives, becomes directional |
| **Light reach** | Extending planet atmosphere toward the source | Other world either responds or doesn't |
| **Medium reach** | More deliberate, sustained extension | Resonance deepens or fades |
| **Hold** | Maintaining resonance without collapsing it | Rift begins to form |
| **Release** | Withdrawing — either gently (let it breathe) or hard (close it) | Rift stabilises or closes |
| **Inverted reach** | Actively withdrawing resonance from an existing rift | Deliberate closure — permanent |

### Connection Map
- **Mutual resonance** → rift forms → connection
- **One-sided reach, no return** → reach fades → rejection
- **Resonance then withdrawal** → partial rift collapses → the specific pain of something that almost was
- **Inverted reach on existing rift** → deliberate closure → burning the bridge

## Sound Design Layer

> The Rift Series is silent-film in structure but uses sound to carry emotional states that visual language alone cannot sustain.

| Sonic Element | Function |
|---|---|
| **Island-specific ambient textures** | Each island has a distinct sound environment reflecting its psychological material |
| **Warm core tone** | A sub-bass warmth — felt more than heard — present whenever the core is intact |
| **Rift resonance** | A harmonic tone between two frequencies — heard when two worlds are in resonance |
| **Hollow silence** | The specific absence of the warm core tone after a pact breaks — an audible gap in the mix |
| **Proximity swell** | Rising ambient warmth in one directional channel when another world draws near |
| **Closure sound** | The sound of a rift closing: not violent — a sustained tone that fades to nothing, leaving one frequency where two were |
