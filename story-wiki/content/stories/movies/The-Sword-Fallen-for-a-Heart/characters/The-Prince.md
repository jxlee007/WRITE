# The Prince (Later King)

> **Appears in:** [The Sword Fallen for a Heart](../index.md)

---

## 👤 Profile & Concept

- **External Goal:** Protect his kingdom and secure a meaningful union with the woman he loved in his youth.
- **Internal Need:** To confront the reality that he cannot control all outcomes and that his invincibility was an illusion sustained by others.
- **Defining Flaw:** Blind belief in his unblemished military record and unexamined arrogance regarding his mother's statecraft.
- **Key Dynamic:** Studied at Gurukul in youth; fell in love with a disguised common girl. Years later, learns she is a princess and pursues marriage, only to be held back by his mother during a critical war.

---

## 🏛️ Character Arc

1. **The Unaware Warrior:** Believes his military victories are purely his own command prowess. Ignorant of his mother's pre-emptive spy network.
2. **The Passive Obedience:** When war erupts in the Princess's kingdom, he obeys the Rajmata's command to stay home for state stability—believing it to be a painful duty rather than orchestrated sabotage.
3. **The Invisible Defeat:** Arrives after the war ends to find real losses and a alienated Princess. His people still view him as undefeated, but he carries a permanent internal loss.
4. **The Grounded Sword:** In the final ceremony, he drives his sword into the earth in front of the Rajmata. Publicly interpreted as total submission; privately an act of unspoken defiance and grief.
