# The Princess

> **Appears in:** [The Sword Fallen for a Heart](../index.md)

---

## 👤 Profile & Concept

- **External Goal:** Secure her kingdom's survival and sovereignty amidst escalating external threats.
- **Internal Need:** To stand as an independent leader rather than relying on external saviors or romantic promises.
- **Defining Flaw:** Misjudging the silence of her ally (the Prince) as deliberate abandonment or political apathy.
- **Key Dynamic:** Raised in hiding as a common girl near Gurukul to prevent political kidnapping. Met the Prince without revealing her true lineage.

---

## 🏛️ Agency & Narrative Arc

> [!IMPORTANT]
> The Princess is **never a passive damsel**. She leads defense strategies, manages political crises, and makes pragmatic calculations for her kingdom's survival.

- **Disguised Youth:** Educated under cover; built genuine emotional bond with the Prince prior to assuming royal duties.
- **Active Crisis Commander:** When the Political Ally attacks, she mobilizes forces and commands tactics rather than passively waiting for rescue.
- **The Pragmatic Choice:** In the aftermath of war, she considers the Political Ally's marriage proposal strictly as a state survival calculation.
- **Post-War Distance:** Meets the Prince after the conflict ends, but is too exhausted by war and political reality to accept delayed explanations.
