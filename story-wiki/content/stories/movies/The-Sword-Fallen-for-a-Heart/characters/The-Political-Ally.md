# The Political Ally / Neighboring Power

> **Appears in:** [The Sword Fallen for a Heart](../index.md)

---

## 👤 Faction Profile & Concept

- **External Goal:** Gain strategic access, territory, or resources from the Princess's kingdom.
- **Role in Narrative:** Antagonistic pressure force whose dormant intelligence is activated by the Rajmata.
- **Key Dynamic:** Maintained a long-standing spy network watching the Princess's realm, awaiting an opportunistic opening.

---

## 🏛️ Tactical Function

- **Dormant vs. Active Threat:** Originally possessed catalogued interest; became an active invasion threat only after receiving leaked intelligence via the Rajmata's channels.
- **Dual Pressure Strategy:** Combines military aggression with a political marriage proposal offer, presenting stable access without requiring total annihilation.
