# The Rajmata (Queen Mother)

> **Appears in:** [The Sword Fallen for a Heart](../index.md)

---

## 👤 Profile & Concept

- **External Goal:** Preserve the crown and protect her son's life from covert political destruction.
- **Internal Need:** To reconcile her fierce maternal love with the destructive statecraft required to protect him.
- **Defining Flaw:** Absolute secrecy; inability to trust her son with the raw, dangerous truth of the political world.
- **Key Dynamic:** Former regent who held true state power before her son came of age. Retains deep access to dormant intelligence networks.

---

## 🏛️ Moral Complexity & Antagonist Logic

> [!NOTE]
> The Rajmata is **not a villain**. Her actions are driven entirely by protective maternal instinct. She leaks dormant intelligence to provoke conflict between the Political Ally and the Princess's kingdom to keep her son away from a mortal trap.

- **Knowledge as Armor:** She holds regency intelligence about the Political Ally's long-standing interest in the Princess's realm.
- **The Traitorous Truth:** She can never defend herself or reveal her motives to the Prince. Admitting the truth would expose state intelligence manipulation and destroy public trust in the throne.
- **The Silent Witness:** Present at the final ceremony, she absorbs the full weight of her son's grounded sword, knowing she saved his life at the cost of his love.
