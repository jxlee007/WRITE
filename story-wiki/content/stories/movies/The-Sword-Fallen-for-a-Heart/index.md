# The Sword Fallen for a Heart

> **Logline:** In a kingdom where knowledge is power and power demands silence, a king who has never lost a war loses the one thing he never fought for — because his mother chose to protect him with a lie he will spend his life failing to understand.

---

## 🎭 Overview & Metadata

- **Genre:** Political tragedy / Court drama (Mythological-era India)
- **Setting:** Ancient India, Gurukul education system era, princely kingdom courts
- **Format:** Feature film / Limited series pilot (~42–52 minutes runtime target)
- **Tonal References:** Sanskrit epic tradition (Bhagavad Gita laying down of arms as thematic parallel), Sophoclean tragedy structure
- **Core Dynamic:** Protective maternal love vs. romantic love; tragedy of silence and incomplete information.

---

## 👥 Main Characters

- [The Prince (Later King)](characters/The-Prince.md) — The undefeated monarch whose first loss is private and invisible.
- [The Rajmata (Queen Mother)](characters/The-Rajmata.md) — Former regent who manipulates intelligence to save her son, destroying his trust forever.
- [The Princess](characters/The-Princess.md) — Hidden in youth, active ruler/defender of her kingdom in crisis.
- [The Political Ally](characters/The-Political-Ally.md) — Faction levered by the Rajmata's leaked intelligence.

---

## 🏛️ Narrative & Theme Architecture

### Core Conflict & Moral Dilemma
Every major character acts rationally based on incomplete information. There are no villains—only characters bound by protective silence.

- **Knowledge as Weapon & Burden:** The Rajmata carries state secrets she can never confess without destroying the crown.
- **Invisible Loss:** The King remains undefeated in the eyes of his subject; his defeat is internal and unacknowledged.
- **The Grounded Sword:** The final gesture in ceremony serves as public deference while carrying private defiance and grief.

---

## 🎬 Act Structure (3-Act Blueprint)

### Act I: Setup & Separation (~12–15 min)
- **Cold Open:** Rajmata unearths dormant regency-era intelligence regarding the Political Ally's interest in the Princess's kingdom.
- **Gurukul Flashback:** Prince meets disguised Princess; mutual connection forged in simplicity.
- **Inciting Incident:** Friend's wedding. Prince discovers the "common girl" is a princess.

### Act II: Collision & Sabotage (~20–25 min)
- **Warning & Dismissal:** Prince informs Rajmata; she warns him of unseen enemies.
- **Abrupt Departure:** Prince visits Princess; urgent crisis forces immediate return before resolving feelings.
- **Maternal Escalation:** Rajmata leaks dormant intelligence to trigger conflict between Political Ally and Princess's realm.
- **Midpoint:** War erupts. Rajmata orders Prince to stay for stability. Princess actively organizes defenses.

### Act III: Reckoning & Silence (~10–12 min)
- **Aftermath:** Prince arrives too late. Battlefield confrontation with a changed, exhausted Princess.
- **Climax:** Public ceremony where the Prince stabs his sword into the ground before the Rajmata.
- **Ending State:** Permanent unresolved silence. Prince lives with painful ambiguity.
