# Chapter 001 — The Dormant Scroll

**Location:** The Inner Archives, Royal Citadel
**POV Character:** The Rajmata (Queen Mother)
**Purpose:** Establish the Rajmata unearthing dormant regency intelligence upon detecting her son's romantic interest, setting in motion the silent protective sabotage.

---

The oil lamp sputtered in the alcove, casting long, wavering shadows across cedar shelves stacked with silk-wrapped parchments. Outside, the midnight wind rattled the iron grates of the citadel, carrying the faint, distant rhythm of ceremonial drums from the outer courtyard.

A heavy wooden box, untouched since the final year of the regency, creaked open under trembling fingers. Dust rose in a thin gray cloud, settling over gold-embroidered cuffs.

Deep in the chest lay a single leather-bound cylinder, sealed with cracked green wax bearing the faded crest of the Southern Alliance. 

The cold brass clasp yielded with a sharp click. Inside, the parchment unrolled under heavy thumbs, revealing row after row of precise, ciphered script written twelve winters ago—a catalog of troop movements, trade debts, and border disputes concerning the neighboring realm.

A single entry near the bottom margin stood highlighted in red ink: a dormant pact detailing the Political Ally's unfulfilled ambition to seize the eastern mountain passes.

The wind outside dropped into a sudden, suffocating silence. 

The Rajmata's pulse thrummed heavily against her collarbone. A breath caught in her chest, sharp as shattered glass.

Her hand smoothed the parchment flat against the dark teak table. 

Footsteps echoed down the stone corridor outside—crisp, measured, unhurried. The King's boots. The tread of a man who had never stepped onto a field of defeat.

The parchment rolled shut with a dry rustle. Slid beneath the velvet folds of her cloak, the leather cylinder disappeared into shadow just as the heavy oak doors swung inward.

"Mother," the Prince's voice cut through the stillness, bright with unburdened pride. "The council has adjourned."

The Rajmata turned toward the light, her countenance smooth as polished marble, hiding the silent storm beneath.

"Then let us speak of the realm's future, my son," she replied, her voice soft, impenetrable, and cold.
