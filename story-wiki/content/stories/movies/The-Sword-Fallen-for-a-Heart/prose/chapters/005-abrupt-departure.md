# Chapter 005 — Abrupt Departure

**Location:** Royal Gardens, Eastern Kingdom  
**Characters:** The Prince, The Princess, Royal Messenger  
**Purpose:** Reconnect the Prince and Princess as equals, establishing mutual connection before an urgent crisis forces his sudden, unresolved departure.  
**Structure:** Proactive Scene (Goal: Reconnect and propose union $\rightarrow$ Conflict: Arrival of urgent border emergency dispatch $\rightarrow$ Disaster: Forced abrupt departure without spoken resolution).

## 📜 Chapter Prose

The white jasmine arbors of the Eastern palace gardens opened onto a wide stone terrace overlooking the river valley below. Evening mist gathered in the hollows, turning the distant pine forests into soft blue silhouettes.

The Princess stood by the carved stone balustrade. She wore the formal silk mantle of her house, but her posture retained the sharp, grounded posture of the girl from the riverbank.

"You took your time finding the road east, King," she said, her back to him as his boots sounded against the gravel path.

The Prince stopped three paces away. The formal titles felt heavy between them, but beneath the silk robes, the silence held the exact same weight as the afternoon by the Gurukul river.

"The road was clear," he said softly. "The timing was not."

She turned slowly, her eyes meeting his. For a long moment, neither spoke. The distance of years seemed to evaporate in the cool evening breeze. He reached into his belt, his fingers touching the small wooden talisman he had carried since his student days.

He took a step forward, his voice dropping into raw honesty. "I did not come to negotiate trade routes."

Before she could answer, heavy bootsteps shattered the quiet of the garden terrace.

A royal messenger, soaked in mud and panting for breath, stumbled onto the stone terrace, falling to one knee before the Prince. He held out a blood-stained leather dispatch tube bearing the royal red seal of the Prince's home citadel.

"My Lord!" the messenger gasped. "Urgent dispatch from the Council. The southern border outposts... emergency measures declared. The Rajmata demands your immediate return before midnight!"

The Prince's hand froze over his belt. The color drained from his face as he stared at the red wax seal.

The Princess watched him, her expression hardening instantly. The warmth in her eyes retreated behind a cool, defensive veil.

"Your kingdom calls," she said softly, her voice devoid of emotion.

"I will return," he said, stepping back toward the messenger, his eyes locked on hers. "Understand me—this is not the end of this conversation."

"The road back is long, King," she replied, stepping back toward the balustrade as he mounted his waiting horse. "Do not make promises your kingdom will not let you keep."

He turned his horse and galloped out of the palace courtyard into the gathering dark, leaving the sentence unfinished in the night air.
