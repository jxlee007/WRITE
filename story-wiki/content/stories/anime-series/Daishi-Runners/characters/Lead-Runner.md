## The Adaptation — What Changes, What Stays

| Rengoku's DNA | Our Lead |
|---------------|----------|
| Fire element | ✅ Kept |
| Explosive, charismatic presence | ✅ Kept |
| Strict moral code | ✅ Kept |
| Philosophy from a parent | ✅ Kept — adapted |
| Broken father figure | ✅ Kept — adapted |
| Self-taught / defied expectations | ✅ Kept |
| Joy / optimism as core expression | ❌ Changed → **Anger** |
| Dies heroically, passes the torch | ❌ Changed → *chooses* sacrifice, is rescued |
| Refuses the "become a demon" offer | ✅ Kept in spirit |

**The key difference:** Rengoku's fire is expressed as *joy*. Our Lead's fire is expressed as *anger*. These are the same element, two different stages. Rengoku already mastered his flame. Our Lead is learning to. The arc is not learning to stop being angry — it is learning to burn *toward* something instead of *at* everything.

## Core Traits

- Loud, direct, physically overwhelming presence — the room changes when he enters
- Anger is always near the surface — not cruelty, but *heat*. He cares too much and it comes out sideways
- Uncompromising moral code — once he decides something is right, he does not move
- Protective to a fault — will throw himself between a teammate and danger without calculating the cost
- Has not yet learned that letting others carry weight is not weakness
- Cannot stand injustice — the Disk responds strongest when he witnesses something *wrong*

## Backstory

### The Parent's Philosophy
His parent (mother or father — TBD) gave him a principle before dying or becoming unreachable:

> *"Those who burn brightest have a duty to light the way for others. Never let that fire go out — but never let it burn what it should be protecting."*

The first half he understood immediately. The second half he is still learning.

### The Broken Father
His father was once a Daishi Runner — possibly a Fire Runner himself. Something broke him:
- **Possible origin (TBD):** touched by evil energy, not fully himself anymore — his dismissiveness and bitterness may not be entirely his own; there may be a corruption thread running underneath

The father told him he had no discipline. That anger was not an emotion you could build on — that the Disk would never respond to rage.

He was wrong. The Disk responds. But the father planted a doubt that the Lead still carries: *what if he's right about the control part?*

### Self-Made
Like Rengoku, he trained himself — or with minimal guidance. He reached Runner level not through the clan's formal path but through sheer relentless drilling. Some in the clan respect this. Some resent it.

### Origin in the Team (Mixed Clan)
TBD — born-in clan or admitted outsider? Given the anger at injustice and the self-made arc, **outsider admitted under the Grand Master's new policy** is the stronger choice. He is one of the first. He has something to prove and people inside the order who want him to fail.

## The Emotional Key — Anger and Fire

The Disk responds to his anger. But anger uncontrolled is just destruction.

**Stages of his arc:**

```
Stage 1 — Raw
  Disk summons, but the power is erratic.
  He wins through force, not mastery.
  Allies get caught in the edges.

Stage 2 — Suppressed (Wrong answer)
  He tries to stop being angry to control the Disk.
  The Disk weakens. He becomes less, not more.
  A mentor or teammate calls this out.

Stage 3 — Directed
  He stops trying to kill the anger and starts asking: what is it FOR?
  Anger at injustice. Anger when allies fall. Anger that the world allows the things it allows.
  The Disk becomes steady, sustained, ferocious with purpose.

Stage 4 — Mastered (End state)
  The fire doesn't go out. It never was supposed to.
  "Set your heart ablaze" — not at enemies, but toward what you protect.
```

## The Sacrifice Moment — His Rengoku Beat

At a critical point in the story — likely against a Tier 1 sub-villain — the team is losing. There is no path where everyone survives.

He chooses to be the pawn. Deliberately. He holds the line so the others can escape, knowing he cannot.

He is not afraid. He is clear. This is what the fire is for.

**Then the Grand Master arrives.**

She comes with reinforcements — the full weight of the Daishi order behind her. And she breaks the siege.

He survives.

> What the Grand Master carries from Dragger's night — the burnt tree, the confession undelivered, the girl who ran and arrived too late — becomes the reason she never lets that happen again on her watch.
> She ran for Dragger. She ran for him. This time she was not too late.

**What this creates for the Lead Runner afterward:**
He prepared to die. He made peace with it. He was *ready*.
And then he didn't.
He has to figure out what it means to still be here — and whether the fire that was ready to go out still burns the same way it did before.
