## Personality & Core Traits

- **The Reluctant Coward:** Constantly panicked, highly vocal about his fear, and convinced each engagement will be fatal. Severely insecure among trained martial prodigies.
- **The Modder / Artisan:** Background in relic-smithing and craftsmanship. Views the spiritual arts through an artisan's eye—molding, adapting, and tweaking rather than clinging to rigid ceremonial form.
- **Deep Loyalty Under Dread:** Despite complaining and trembling, he will throw himself into danger when comrades are backed into a corner.

---

## Power & Combat: The Lost Ancestor Art

### 1. The Pure Energy Disk
Unlike the traditional elemental runners (Fire, Water, Air, Earth), his Disk channels raw, untethered spiritual energy. Because it is unaligned, he can tweak its density, shape, and resonance on the fly.

### 2. Spectral Limbs & Armored Titan (Komamura Influence)
When he spins his Disk, he manifests a forgotten Daishi discipline:
- **Limb Manifestation:** Colossal phantom gauntlets and spectral blade-arms materialize in the air, mirroring his rapid strikes to crush heavy armor or deflect massive assaults.
- **Full Manifestation (The Colossal Ancestor):** A towering, fully armored ancient Daishi samurai guardian forms around him, swinging titanic weapons in tandem with his motions.

### 3. The Forgotten Art & Natural Affinity
Centuries ago, the founders of the Daishi clan used this titan projection. It was eventually abandoned and erased from active practice because the physical and spiritual toll broke ordinary bodies. To the horror and awe of the clan's traditionalists, this anxious outsider channels this lost primordial art purely by instinct.

### 4. The Critical Vulnerability: Mirrored Damage
- Any blow landed on the spectral giant is **simultaneously reflected onto his own physical body**.
- Because he is acutely terrified of agony and death, he resists summoning the avatar while conscious.
- **The Trigger:** When mortal panic spikes past tolerance, his conscious mind shuts down into **waking tunnel-vision**. His body moves on pure autonomic survival instinct, unleashing the crushing titan without doubt or hesitation.

## Narrative & Thematic Role

- **The Clan's Greatest Embarrassment & Marvel:** Admitted under the Female Grand Master's reformed outsider policy. The conservative elders mock his cowardice and lack of pedigree, only to be struck dumb when he naturally re-awakens the clan's most sacred, long-lost heritage.
- **Kinship with the Fire Lead:** The Fire Lead burns hot and heads directly into conflict; the Wildcard frantically searches for exits. Yet when the Lead overextends, the Wildcard's instinctual giant is often the only thing massive enough to block the fatal blow.
