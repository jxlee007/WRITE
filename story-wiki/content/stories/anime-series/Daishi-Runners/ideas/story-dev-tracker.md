## What Is Missing ❌

### 1. Protagonist(s)
- Who is the lead Runner?
- How many Runners are on the team?
- What are their elements / emotional keys?
- What is the lead's personal arc — what do they *want*, what are they *wrong about*?
- How does the lead relate to the clan (born-in or the first outsider admitted under the new policy)?

### 2. Top Dog — Main Villain
- Name?
- What does he want — and why?
- What is his power / how does he differ from the sub-villains?
- Does he have a tragic past, or is he purely a force of evil?
- What is his relationship to the Disk system — does he understand it, fear it, want to corrupt it?

### 3. Villain Hierarchy (The "Twelve Moons" equivalent)
Currently we have: **Dragger** (cursed, non-loyal, side arc)
Need: a ranked structure of demon sub-villains each with distinct power, personality, possible tragic backstory
- How many tiers? (Demon Slayer has Upper and Lower — 6 + 6)
- Are they loyal to Top Dog or do some have their own agendas?
- Do any have redemption arcs, or are they all defeated and gone?

### 4. Story Structure
- How many arcs / seasons?
- What is the **central dramatic question** of the whole story?
- How does the Dragger side arc fit into the main timeline?
- Is the Female Grand Master a supporting character to the protagonists, or does she get her own episodes?

### 5. World Rules (Gaps)
- What is the demon world called / what does it look like?
- Can demons enter the human world freely, or is there a condition?
- What happens to a Disk if a Runner dies?
- Are there more elements beyond the base four?

## Build Order (Recommended)

```
1. Protagonist(s)         ← need this before anything else can be written
2. Top Dog                ← the final boss shapes the whole story
3. Villain Hierarchy      ← fills the arcs between start and finale
4. Story Structure        ← arcs, episodes, dramatic question
5. World Rules            ← fill gaps as they become relevant
```
