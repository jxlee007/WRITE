## Premise

The **Daishi Runners** (also called *Daishi Ninja*) are an order of sacred swordsmen — feared, revered, and few. Each Runner holds a **mythical Elemental Disk**, a relic bound to a natural force.

## Core Mechanic — The Disk Spin

1. **Default combat:** Runners fight with conventional sword technique (Schwarz style).
2. **Threshold trigger:** When evil exceeds a critical power threshold, the Runner's **emotional state** resonates with their Disk.
3. **Summoning:** The Disk materialises in the Runner's hand.
4. **Activation:** The Disk is slotted around the sword hilt and **spun / rotated**.
5. **Channelling:** While the spin continues, the Disk pulls elemental power through the blade into the physical world.
6. **Resolution:** The amplified power is used to overcome the greater evil — truth/justice wins.

> **Tone note:** Inspired by *Power Rangers Samurai* visually/mechanically, but the story aims for a darker villain arc (Demon Slayer-style) and a deeper protagonist emotional journey.
