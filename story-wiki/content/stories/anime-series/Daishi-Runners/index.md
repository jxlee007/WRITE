## The Runners — Team of Five

Each Runner carries one elemental Disk, summoned through a specific emotional resonance. The team is **mixed** — some born into the Daishi clan, some the first outsiders admitted under the Female Grand Master's reformed policy. That tension is internal story fuel.

## Villain Structure

### Top Dog — Main Villain
- **Core nature:** Ideological + Ancient — not tragic, *certain*
- He knows emotions summon Disks. He thinks that is a flaw.
- Has spent centuries engineering demons who do not feel, to counter the Daishi system
- The Dragger trap was not cruelty — it was proof of concept: *loyalty is your vulnerability*
- Cannot be reasoned with, cannot be redeemed — he is not broken, he is **wrong**

## World Rules

- Disk summoning requires emotional resonance — the emotion must be genuine and strong
- Performing the ritual unshielded (no master present) exposes the practitioner to ambient evil energy
- Night = peak evil energy — worst time for an unshielded ritual
- A corrupted Disk can be freed if the original emotion overpowers the bound evil energy (proven by Dragger's ending)
- Disk power that exceeds normal range can permanently block/eliminate evil — not just suppress (new, proven once)
