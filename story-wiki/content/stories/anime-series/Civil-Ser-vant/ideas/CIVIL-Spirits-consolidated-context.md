# SECTION 1: STORY PROGRESSION & WORLD-BUILDING

## Base Setting (CIVIL)
- Near-future India governed by **CIVILNET**, an omnipresent AI mesh
- City is vertically stratified: **Upstack** (wealthy, privileged) vs. **Downstack** (poor, surveilled, exploited)
- Currency: **Universal Basic Credits (UBC)** — not rupees. Mesh-administered UBI (~300-400 UBC/month baseline for Downstack residents). All UBC transactions are tracked by the Mesh; this is a control mechanism.
- Pilot protagonist: "The Awakened" — a youth with experimental neural firmware, discovering CIVILNET's Decree mechanics (patrol cycles, Protocol Windows) as the world's core rule-system
- Secondary protagonist: "Craze" (ex-spy contractor) — mysterious observer, badge marked "007," first seen at a transit platform bench, makes eye contact and a near-imperceptible nod with the Awakened. Relationship/history unconfirmed.
- Two-hander structure: 70% Awakened arc / 30% Craze's parallel operations in the pilot

## The Spirits Faction — Origin & Purpose
- Full name in-world: **"The Spirits" / "The Collective"** — deliberately diffuse, no unified single name
- **Inspired by:** Avatar: The Last Airbender's spirit mechanics — invisible, pervasive, acting outside normal political structures, neither wholly good nor evil
- **Core philosophy:** *"It's not about who is making the shots, it's about who is calling the shots."* — power isn't force, it's control of the framework in which decisions matter
- **The irony (central thematic tension):** The Spirits diagnose the Mesh's method of control (framework control, not force) correctly — but then replicate it. The Mesh says "obey or be enforced." The Spirits say "I saved you, so now you belong to me" (see Venus). Both eliminate agency; only the packaging differs.

## Why They're Called "Spirits" (Naming Origin — CONFIRMED)
- **Public/Downstack belief:** The Spirits aren't human — they have no soul, don't feel, exist outside normal moral rules, appear from shadows, vanish without trace like ghosts. This mythology makes them unnegotiable and unfightable — "you can't reason with a spirit, you can only survive it."
- **The actual truth:** This is the OPPOSITE of internal reality for the good members. Members like Badger, Languard, and Sonar are deeply human — they carry guilt, doubt, and moral weight.
- **The mechanism:** The faction's *bad* members are the ones who act genuinely without conscience, and their behavior is what "proves" the public's soulless-ghost mythology correct. Their excessive, often tactically unnecessary cruelty maintains the fear that protects the whole faction (fear = leverage; fear = the public not resisting or negotiating).
- **Note:** Specific bad-member characters/profiles have NOT been defined yet and should not be invented without explicit instruction. Only the *concept* (good members hide behind bad members' reputation) is confirmed.

## The Black Market: Illegal Biological Enhancement / Organ Trade
- The Spirits run an illegal black market in biological enhancements and **natural human organs**, operating across India
- **Core economic premise:** Natural/organic organs are treated as **luxury/exotic goods** for the rich; synthetic organs are the affordable "standard" for everyone else
- **Approximate black market pricing (in UBC):**
  - Heart: 50,000 UBC (vs. 2,000 UBC synthetic) — ~25x markup
  - Liver: 25,000 UBC (vs. 1,000 UBC) — ~25x
  - Kidney: 12,000 UBC (vs. 500 UBC) — ~24x
  - Lung: 18,000 UBC (vs. 750 UBC) — ~24x
  - Eyes (pair): 8,000 UBC (vs. 300 UBC) — ~26x
  - Bone Marrow: 3,000 UBC (vs. 120 UBC) — ~25x
- **The trap:** An organ sale = 30-40 months of a Downstack worker's wages. Donors receive only ~15% of sale value (e.g., a 12,000 UBC kidney sale nets the donor ~1,800 UBC) — the rest goes to the faction's operations, personnel, and reserves.
- **The Mesh's role:** The Mesh doesn't stop this black market — it is *structurally necessary* to the Mesh's system. By making synthetic organs inadequate and natural organs inaccessible to the poor, the Mesh manufactures the desperation that feeds the black market. The black market functions as a "pressure valve" that prevents organized Downstack rebellion — as long as there's a path to survival via organ sale, there's less appetite for uprising. The Mesh and black market are effectively **codependent**, not opposed.
- **Supply chain roles (general structure, not yet cast with specific named characters beyond leadership):** runners (recruit/approach desperate sellers), surgeons (black-market medical staff), couriers (transport organs, evade Mesh checkpoints), buyers (wealthy Upstack elites, medical tourists, underground hospitals, sometimes Mesh-connected elites themselves)
- **Origin of Viper & Badger's involvement:** They did not choose this path ideologically — it began as a **survival mechanism**. Orphaned young in the Downstack (parents died from inability to afford organ care), they organized what was already a chaotic, predatory black market into something more structured — better pay for donors, lower surgical mortality, more professional standards — believing this made them "less predatory" than the status quo. Over time this organizing made them *more* efficient at exploitation even as they told themselves they were the lesser evil.
- **The central moral tragedy (confirmed thematic throughline):** The Spirits are correct that the Mesh exploits through controlling frameworks — but the Spirits have built an identical framework of their own (the organ black market, faction loyalty structures) and now call the shots for people who still have no real choices. This is the story's core irony and should inform major arcs.

## The "Who Calls the Shots" Philosophy — Layers
1. **Mesh layer:** Controls governance, law, enforcement, official medical systems
2. **Spirits layer:** Controls the black market — organ pricing, surgeon standards, operative recruitment, distribution
3. **Individual operative layer:** Each operative controls their own operations/decisions within the faction structure
- Each layer claims to "liberate" the layer below it, but control remains control regardless of framing (rescue, liberation, chosen family, professionalization).

# SECTION 2: CHARACTER DEVELOPMENTS

## VIPER (Deceased — Founder, appears via flashback/memory/legacy)
- **Real name:** Redacted/unrevealed
- **Age at death:** 28
- **Status:** Murdered ~3 weeks before the Awakened's emergence in the main story; safe house in Downstack Sector 7-C, only known to him and Badger
- **Cause of death:** Infected via **Mesh-viral code** delivered by a trap informant — a weapon designed to trigger his self-healing response and spread faster through it, effectively weaponizing his own regeneration
- **Last act:** Called Badger before dying to tell her: (1) the Mesh is developing anti-Spirits weapons, (2) someone inside the faction leaked his safe house location, (3) she is now in charge whether she wants it or not. Then destroyed evidence to disguise the true cause of death.
- **Unsolved mystery (active plot thread):** Who inside the Spirits betrayed Viper's location? This drives investigation subplots for multiple characters.
- **Visual/tech design:** Costume inspired by Free Fire's Viper skin (blue/red serpentine tactical suit) and Brawl Stars' Edgar/Orschu (acrobatic, self-healing playstyle)
- **Artificial Skin Technology (AST):** Bio-synthetic integrated shield/regeneration system —
  - Tier 1: Passive accelerated healing, minor wounds in hours
  - Tier 2: Adaptive plasma-membrane shield vs. lasers/firearms (15-20 sec duration, 30-40 sec cooldown)
  - Tier 3: Rapid limb reattachment/knitting (~60 sec, painful, 80% functional recovery immediately)
  - Weakness: requires constant power; without it, AST is inert
- **Personality:** Hyperlogical, patient, serpentine — waits and strikes at maximum vulnerability. Not ideological; motivated by leverage, not liberation rhetoric. Protective of Badger as his one illogical exception.
- **The Rule:** "Don't f*** with Badger" — an absolute faction law he enforced without exception; its origin traces to an early betrayal where a compromised operative sold out Badger's location, after which Viper erased that operative's entire existence as a warning. The rule functions as Viper's only expressible form of love/care.
- **Relationship to Badger:** Siblings (biological or found-family — ambiguous), grew up together in Downstack. Drifted into separate orbits as adults (he ran faction violence, she pursued biology/research) but he protected her from a distance throughout.

## BADGER / REL (Relwrop) — Current Faction Leader
- **Real name:** Relwrop, goes by "Rel"
- **Pen name/faction name:** Badger
- **Age:** 25 (24 at Viper's death)
- **Status:** Active; rose to faction leadership after Viper's death, Series 2 onward
- **Before Viper's death:** "The Biologist" — specialized researcher, minimal faction politics, deliberately distanced herself from Viper's violent operations via compartmentalization/dissociation
- **The transition:** Learning of Viper's death caused 72 hours of non-functional shock; when she resumed, her emotional compartmentalization had collapsed. She took command not because she wanted power but because the faction was fracturing and needed a Viper-adjacent authority.
- **Leadership style:** Cold, ruthless clarity — colder than Viper because she had to *learn* detachment rather than being naturally wired for it. Consolidated authority partly by testing doubters with dangerous assignments rather than executing them outright (some doubters died on these tests; some survived and became loyal).
- **Core drives:** (1) Vengeance — obsessively investigating who killed Viper and why; this investigation increasingly competes with her faction leadership duties. (2) Biological obsession — studying the Mesh-viral code that killed Viper, both for vengeance and because it's her original area of expertise.
- **Visual design:** Full-length black technical coat (temperature-regulated, sleeves to mid-finger), matte black nail coating (bio-compatible, improves nano-tech activation speed ~8%), single braid, very dark almost-black eyes
- **Nano-Tech Blade System (NTBS):** Bio-integrated nano-tech in fingernails —
  - Standby: nails appear normal/matte black, she looks unarmed
  - Activation (2-3 sec): nano-structures expand, harden to crystalline diamond-hardness, extrude as angled (30-45°) serrated pink/magenta blades, 3-5 inches long, audible "zing" on extension
  - Uses: slashing, piercing, climbing (blades as hand/foot holds), multi-strike (all ten fingers simultaneously)
  - Power: ~15%/30 sec continuous combat, ~3%/min holding without striking, 4-6 hr full recharge, emergency overcharge available at higher pain/drain cost
  - Weakness: blades can shatter against hardened steel/advanced alloys; regrowth takes 2-3 weeks
- **Combat style:** Precision Striker/Surgical Assassin — economical movement, single precise disabling/lethal cuts, disengages immediately rather than pressing an advantage (opposite of Viper's overwhelming-force approach)
- **Investigation subplot (active thread):** Who killed Viper — threads include the Mesh-viral code's origin, the internal leak of the safe house location, the informant's true identity/status, and the open question of whether Viper's consciousness could have a backup/ghost-form somewhere in the network

## LANGUARD (Karthik Desai) — Ground Operations Commander
- **Age:** 32 | **Role:** Hand-to-hand combat specialist, de facto second-in-command
- **Cyberskin:** Quake Amplification System (QAS) — gloves and shoes that amplify kinetic force via harmonic resonance (not raw strength increase, but force-transfer efficiency)
  - Gloves: shockwave punches (15-ft radius), calibratable for precision or area-denial strikes, defensive force redirection
  - Shoes: ground-slam quakes, burst-speed mobility, precision footwork, climbing-assist vibration/friction
  - Power: ~8%/strike, ~2%/min defensive hold, 3-4 hr recharge
  - Weakness: needs solid footing; unreliable on ice/water/unstable terrain
- **Personality:** Stillness as strength; unshakeable calm; unconditional loyalty (to Viper, then transferred fully to Badger); tactical genius via simple-but-perfect planning (97% success rate)
- **Relationship notes:** The one person Badger trusts to challenge her decisions when she risks self-destruction. Quietly harbors unconfirmed suspicion that someone in faction leadership may be connected to Viper's death; has not confronted Badger about it.

## KILLER BEE (Priya Sharma) — Intelligence & Infiltration Specialist
- **Age:** 26 | **Role:** Reconnaissance, intelligence gathering, Mesh infiltration
- **CONFIRMED: She is loyal to the Spirits, not a Mesh mole.** She actively infiltrates Mesh facilities *on behalf of* the faction, reporting directly to Badger.
- **Cyberskin:** Resonance Shield Array (RSA) with Harmonic Wing Configuration
  - Standby: near-invisible heat-haze shimmer
  - Full sphere mode: ~15-ft protective sphere, reinforced-concrete-equivalent density, survives ballistic/thermal/concussive attacks, can survive a nuclear blast at close range (~100m), lasts 45-60 sec, charges from absorbed impacts
  - Wing transformation: sphere converts to two geometric wings retaining accumulated charge; offensive energy-discharge strikes (3-5 before depleting); gliding/parachute mobility (no sustained flight/altitude gain, only controlled descent and lateral glide)
  - Stealth veil mode: translucent camouflage + sound dampening, 20-30 min duration, breaks on aggressive action
  - Power: sphere ~20%/30sec, wing strike ~15%/discharge, stealth ~8%/min, 2-3 hr recharge
- **Personality:** Master of emotional flexibility/deception as tradecraft; isolation by design; predatory patience. Uses beauty/appearance as a deliberate weaponized tool for infiltration.
- **Revised arc focus:** NOT a betrayal/loyalty-question arc. Her actual internal conflict is **identity erosion from extended deep-cover immersion in Mesh systems** — the risk that after months embedded as a Mesh-facing persona, she may lose track of which identity (Priya Sharma / Spirits operative vs. her cover persona) is "real." Badger has noticed early signs (flattening affect, narrowing emotional range, mechanical speech patterns) but hasn't addressed it, partly because Killer Bee is too valuable to pull from operations.
- **Relationship notes:** Respected Viper deeply, possibly unrequited feelings, never acted on.

- **Ages:** Larson 29, Law 27 | **Role:** Firmware engineering, network architecture/hacking, near-inseparable operational pair
- **Larson ("The Architect"):** Tall, thin, cybernetic left-eye implant for real-time code interface; neon-accented clothing with embedded displays; jerky/unpredictable physical movement
- **Law ("The Tactician"):** Compact, muscular, code-pattern tattoo sleeve on right arm; practical tactical wear; precise economical movement
- **Distributed Glitch Processing System (DGPS):**
  - Local mode: Larson's backpack quantum processor (real-time encryption-breaking, exploit generation) + Law's wrist network interface (environmental system hacking — cameras, doors, traffic controls — for diversions/escape routes)
  - Remote mode: firmware hijacking of bio-interfaces, cascading network failures, false-signature "ghost presence" to evade detection, mass data exfiltration
  - Resonant Link (twin sync, within ~50m): combined/multiplicative processing power, shared real-time tactical data, redundancy if one is captured
  - Minor physical augmentation: reflex boosters (digital-threat reaction speed), tactical pain suppression, metabolic optimization (used to dangerous excess — health risk)
  - Power: Larson ~15%/min active hacking, Law ~8%/min surveillance/diversion, combined ~25%/min, 2 hr recharge
- **Combat note:** NOT combat-effective in direct physical confrontation; rely entirely on environmental hacking, tactical retreat, and support role for field operatives like Languard/Badger
- **Personality:** Obsessive problem-solvers who cannot walk away from unsolved technical challenges (has gotten them captured before); emotionally blind to non-technical concepts (loyalty, fear, loss understood intellectually, not felt); treat hacking as play even when stakes are lethal; both privately fear their consciousness may already be partially distributed into the Mesh's network from so much interfacing.

## SONAR (Rashmit "Rash" Patel) — Tactical Demolitions / Acoustic Warfare
- **Age:** 30 | **Role:** Demolitions, area denial, ex-military operative
- **Cyberskin:** Modular Subsonic Resonance Pack (MSRP), backpack-mounted, transforms into shoulder-mounted weapon system
  - Tractor-beam function: directional subsonic force fields — push/pull/knock down opponents (up to ~30m range), create temporary force barriers
  - Rotatable "tank-gun" mounting: 360° swivel emitter array, burst-fire mode (3-5 pulses/sec), targeting HUD
  - Mobility use: downward-directed force for 10-15 ft vertical jumps, forward force for burst sprinting, upward force for controlled/slowed falls
  - Power: ~12%/20sec tractor mode, ~8%/5 bursts, ~10%/30sec mobility, 2.5-3 hr recharge
  - Limitations: reduced effectiveness in enclosed spaces, ineffective/unpredictable underwater, counterable by sound-dampening tech (e.g., Killer Bee's RSA), prolonged use causes hearing/organ damage (Sonar already has partial deafness in left ear from early experimentation, compensated by an implanted directional audio processor)
- **Personality:** Formal military discipline and protocol (distinct from the faction's criminal/scientific origins of other members); pragmatic and direct — applies exactly the force an objective requires, no more, no less; respects chain of command; has a younger sister still in Downstack (partial motivation for joining — funding her education, making the city safer for her)
- **Internal conflict:** The operative most likely to have quiet moral doubts about specific operations while still following orders; independently investigating Viper's death outside of Badger's sanctioned investigation.

## VENUS (Vanya Kovic) — Adaptive Combat Specialist
- **Age:** 24 | **Role:** Field operative, newest full member, aggressive assault/threat specialist
- **CONFIRMED origin:** She was a Mesh biological research subject — part of an organ-rejection/replacement experimentation program, caught mid-forced-transformation. The Spirits (Killer Bee-led operation) extracted her from a Mesh facility, rescuing her from what would otherwise have been death or permanent enslavement to the experiment.
- **CONFIRMED loyalty:** Because the Spirits literally saved her life, she has **complete, unwavering devotion** to the faction and especially to Badger. This is not framed as instability — it is framed as absolute loyalty with dangerous implications (she will execute any order without question, including self-sacrifice, and will not push back on faction ethics even when something seems wrong).
- **Visual design:** Lithe/muscular build, visible augmentation scars on legs/lower torso (worn without shame), minimal upper-body clothing to showcase mobility, reinforced tactical pants housing the lower-body armor system, unusual technologically-sheened pupils
- **Integrated Kinetic Adaptive System (IKAS):** Lower-body transformation tech, aesthetic inspired by **Airachnid's biomechanical transformation from Transformers Prime**
  - Partial activation: leg armor plating deploys (composite/metallic lattice exoskeleton), boosts mobility (speed, jump height 10-15ft) and durability, wall-mounting/climbing contact points
  - Advanced activation: torso plating extends, more insectoid silhouette, improved aerial stability, hardened strike surfaces
  - Full transformation: six-legged biomechanical combat form, ~2x normal height, ~300% lift-capacity increase, dramatically increased speed/maneuverability across all terrain orientations (horizontal/vertical/inverted), iridescent green/purple/black color shift, intentionally psychologically intimidating
  - Power: partial ~5%/min, advanced ~8%/min, full ~15%/min, 3-4 hr recharge, transformation reversal faster (~10sec) than activation (~20sec)
  - Psychological cost: extended full-transformation (30+ min) shifts her cognition toward predatory/apex-hunter thinking, reduced empathy, requires deactivation/recovery period; managed via medication developed by the Glitch Bros, which she sometimes skips because she prefers the enhanced predatory clarity (an unaddressed slow-motion risk)
- **Combat style:** Hyper-mobile striker/adaptive predator — enters with visible aggression to psychologically destabilize, closes distance fast, adapts tactics to opponent type, persists until incapacitation or forced disengagement
- **Central tension (confirmed thematic use, not yet dramatized):** Her devotion is *chosen*, not coerced — but this raises the question of what Badger owes someone whose entire existence now depends on her moral judgment. This is framed as a leadership burden for Badger rather than a defect in Venus.
