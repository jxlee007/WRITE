# SONAR

**Story:** [Civil Ser-vant](../index.md)
**Type:** Character
**Status:** Active
**Role:** Tactical Demolitions & Acoustic Warfare — the Spirits
**Real name:** Rashmit "Rash" Patel
**Age:** 30

---

## Identity

Sonar is the Spirits' most formally trained operative — ex-military, discipline-grounded, protocol-respecting. He arrived in the faction through a path different from its scientific and criminal origins: a professional soldier who ended up in an organization of biologists, hackers, and trauma survivors. He is the operative most likely to have quiet moral doubts about a specific operation. He follows orders anyway, and then independently investigates the decisions he followed.

---

## Core Traits

- **Formal military discipline:** Distinct from the faction's criminal and scientific backgrounds. He has a chain-of-command reflex that exists independent of whether he agrees with the command.
- **Pragmatic and direct:** Applies exactly the force an objective requires, no more and no less. Not theatrical about violence.
- **Respects chain of command:** Will raise concerns through legitimate channels before acting on personal judgment. Has not yet found the limit of this. May be approaching it.
- **Partial deafness:** Left ear, from early experimentation with the MSRP. Compensated by an implanted directional audio processor. He doesn't mention it.

---

## Modular Subsonic Resonance Pack (MSRP)

Backpack-mounted acoustic warfare system that transforms into a shoulder-mounted weapon array:

| Function | Capability |
|----------|-----------|
| **Tractor-beam** | Directional subsonic force fields — push, pull, or knock down opponents at up to ~30m range |
| **Force barriers** | Temporary subsonic barriers for area denial or cover |
| **Rotatable emitter array** | 360° swivel, burst-fire mode (3–5 pulses/sec), integrated targeting HUD |
| **Mobility** | Downward-directed force for 10–15 ft vertical jumps; forward force for burst sprinting; upward force for controlled descent |

**Power consumption:** ~12%/20 sec tractor mode | ~8%/5 bursts | ~10%/30 sec mobility  
**Recharge:** 2.5–3 hours

**Limitations:**
- Reduced effectiveness in enclosed spaces (resonance scatter, unpredictable reflection)
- Ineffective and unpredictable underwater
- Counterable by sound-dampening technology (e.g., Killer Bee's RSA stealth veil creates interference)
- Prolonged use at high intensity risks organ and hearing damage — Sonar has already paid this cost once

## Personal Stakes

A younger sister still living in Downstack. Part of his motivation for joining the Spirits was funding her education and, in a more diffuse way, making the city less hostile to people like her. He has not fully reckoned with what the faction actually does to Downstack residents via the black market. This reckoning is deferred rather than avoided.

---

## Internal Conflict

The operative most likely to have quiet moral doubts about specific operations while still executing them. He does not defect; he compartmentalizes. The compartment is getting full.

Independently investigating Viper's death outside of Badger's sanctioned investigation — his angle is operational and tactical rather than biological or political. He wants to know how the informant got close enough, and what failure of protocol made it possible. Whether this investigation eventually intersects with Languard's independent inquiry is an open question.

---

## Relationship Notes

- **Badger:** Respects her authority. Has not yet had reason to test whether that respect has limits.
- **Languard:** Closest professional peer — both ex-operatives with formal training backgrounds, both quietly running unsanctioned threads on Viper's death. They have not yet discovered they are doing the same thing from different angles.
- **Glitch Bros:** Values their technical output. Finds their inability to disengage from problems a liability he mentally accounts for in every plan they're part of.
