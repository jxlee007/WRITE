# LANGUARD

**Story:** [Civil Ser-vant](../index.md)
**Type:** Character
**Status:** Active
**Role:** Ground Operations Commander, de facto second-in-command — the Spirits
**Real name:** Karthik Desai
**Age:** 32

---

## Identity

Languard is the Spirits' most experienced active operative and Badger's de facto second-in-command. His authority is built on a 97% mission success rate, a combat record no other member can match, and an unconditional loyalty that transferred cleanly from Viper to Badger without hesitation or condition. He does not require ideology to be loyal. He requires trust — and once given, it is absolute.

---

## Core Traits

- **Stillness as strength:** He does not move, speak, or act unnecessarily. The stillness is not passive — it is the compression before force.
- **Unconditional loyalty:** First to Viper; now, fully and without reservation, to Badger. This is not blind deference — he will challenge her when she is risk-seeking her own destruction. It is unconditional in the sense that it does not require her to be right.
- **Tactical simplicity:** His mission planning is described as simple-but-perfect. He does not overengineer. He finds the minimum sufficient solution and executes it without variation. 97% success rate.

---

## Quake Amplification System (QAS)

Bio-integrated kinetic amplification via harmonic resonance, housed in gloves and shoes. Does not increase raw physical strength — amplifies force-transfer efficiency:

**Gloves:**
- Shockwave punches with ~15-ft blast radius
- Calibratable between precision (targeted individual) and area-denial (crowd/room)
- Defensive force redirection — incoming kinetic force can be absorbed and redistributed

**Shoes:**
- Ground-slam quakes for area denial and disorientation
- Burst-speed mobility (short distance acceleration)
- Precision footwork and climbing-assist via vibration and friction modulation

**Power consumption:** ~8%/strike | ~2%/min defensive hold  
**Recharge:** 3–4 hours  
**Critical weakness:** Requires solid footing. Significantly reduced or unreliable on ice, water, or unstable terrain.

---

## Relationship Notes

- **Badger:** The one person she allows to push back on her decisions. He does this not as insubordination but as a function of loyalty — he will tell her when she is making a choice that destroys rather than leads. She knows he will; it is part of why she trusts him.
- **Viper:** His loyalty transferred to Badger at the moment of Viper's death without a transition period. This is not emotional flatness — it is a decision he made about where loyalty lives when the person who held it is gone.
- **Private suspicion:** Quietly harbors an unconfirmed suspicion that someone in faction leadership — not Badger — may be connected to Viper's death. He has not confronted anyone about this. He is collecting rather than acting.

---

## Internal Investigation Thread

Conducts an independent, unsanctioned investigation into Viper's death — separate from Badger's sanctioned investigation and not disclosed to her. His angle is the leadership layer, not the Mesh-viral code. He is the slowest-moving thread on this question and the one most likely to arrive at an answer no one wants to hear.
