# BADGER / REL

**Story:** [Civil Ser-vant](../index.md)
**Type:** Character
**Status:** Active — Series 2 onward as faction leader
**Role:** Current Leader, the Spirits
**Real name:** Relwrop, goes by "Rel"
**Faction name:** Badger

---

## Identity

Rel was the Spirits' biologist before she was their leader. She spent her career in the faction's research wing, deliberately compartmentalized from Viper's violent operations — a distance she maintained through deliberate dissociation. When Viper died, that distance collapsed. She took command not because she wanted power but because the faction was fracturing and needed a Viper-adjacent authority to hold it together. She was 24. She is now 25.

---

## Core Traits

- **Learned detachment:** Viper's coldness was innate. Badger's is constructed — she had to teach herself to feel less, which makes her, paradoxically, colder than he was. What comes naturally requires no maintenance; what is learned requires constant reinforcement.
- **Dual obsession:** Vengeance for Viper's death and biological fascination with the Mesh-viral code that killed him. These two drives compete with her faction leadership duties and will eventually conflict with them.
- **Compartmentalization collapsed:** Before Viper's death, she managed the psychological distance between knowing and not-feeling. Afterward, the wall came down. She rebuilt it with colder architecture.

---

## Transition: The 72 Hours

Learning of Viper's death put Rel in 72 hours of non-functional shock. When she resumed, the emotional compartmentalization she had maintained for years had collapsed, and she rebuilt it from scratch — colder, less permeable, and enforced by will rather than habit. She consolidated leadership partly by testing doubters with dangerous assignments rather than executing them outright. Some doubters died on those assignments. Some survived and became loyal. She does not distinguish between the two outcomes.

## Nano-Tech Blade System (NTBS)

Bio-integrated nano-tech in her fingernails:

| State | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| **Standby** | Nails appear normal, matte black coating. She appears unarmed. |
| **Activation (2–3 sec)** | Nano-structures expand, harden to crystalline diamond-hardness, extrude as angled (30–45°) serrated pink/magenta blades, 3–5 inches long. Audible "zing" on extension. |
| **Uses** | Slashing, piercing, climbing (blades as hand/foot holds), multi-strike with all ten fingers simultaneously |

**Power consumption:** ~15%/30 sec continuous combat | ~3%/min holding without striking  
**Recharge:** 4–6 hours full | Emergency overcharge available at higher pain and drain cost  
**Weakness:** Blades can shatter against hardened steel or advanced alloys. Regrowth after shattering takes 2–3 weeks.

**The matte black nail coating** is bio-compatible and improves nano-tech activation speed by approximately 8%.

---

## Combat Style

**Precision Striker / Surgical Assassin.** Economical movement, single precise disabling or lethal cuts, immediate disengagement after landing a strike rather than pressing an advantage. This is the direct opposite of Viper's overwhelming-force approach — a difference in wiring, not just preference.

---

## Core Drives

1. **Vengeance:** Who killed Viper, and how? The investigation — Mesh-viral code origin, the internal faction leak, the informant's true identity — increasingly competes with her leadership duties.
2. **Biological obsession:** The Mesh-viral code that killed Viper is, to her, also a scientific object. Studying it is both vengeance preparation and original expertise. She cannot fully separate the two.

---

## Investigation Subplot (Active Thread)

- The Mesh-viral code's origin and the specific weapon design
- The identity of the internal leak who gave the safe house location to the informant
- The informant's true identity and current status
- The open question of whether Viper's consciousness could have a backup or ghost-form somewhere in the network

Also inherited from Viper: the **tech demonstrator thread** — rumors of someone outmatching automated enforcement robots with just influence. Badger has not yet connected this to the Protagonist but the thread is active.
