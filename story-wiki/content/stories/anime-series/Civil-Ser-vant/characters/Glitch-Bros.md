# GLITCH BROS — LARSON & LAW

**Story:** [Civil Ser-vant](../index.md)
**Type:** Characters (inseparable operational pair)
**Status:** Active
**Role:** Firmware Engineering, Network Architecture & Hacking — the Spirits
**Ages:** Larson (Keene) — 29 | Law (Vass) — 27

---

## Identity

The Glitch Bros are the Spirits' technical infrastructure. Nearly everything the faction does in cyberspace passes through their systems. They function as a pair — operationally, cognitively, and temperamentally — to a degree that makes them practically one distributed system with two bodies. They are not physically effective in direct confrontation, and they do not need to be.

---

## Individual Profiles

### LARSON — "The Architect"
- **Build:** Tall, thin
- **Augmentation:** Cybernetic left-eye implant for real-time code interface — scrolling overlays, active exploit management, environmental code reading
- **Aesthetic:** Neon-accented clothing with embedded displays; wears the work visibly
- **Movement:** Jerky, unpredictable physical movement that has nothing to do with nervousness — it is a side effect of constant micro-adjustments from the eye implant processing real-time data
- **Role:** Quantum processing and encryption-breaking; exploit generation and architecture design

### LAW — "The Tactician"
- **Build:** Compact, muscular
- **Augmentation:** Code-pattern tattoo sleeve on right arm (functional — embedded interface nodes, not decorative)
- **Aesthetic:** Practical tactical wear; no visible displays
- **Movement:** Precise and economical — the physical counterpart to Larson's digital chaos
- **Role:** Environmental system hacking (cameras, doors, traffic controls, facility infrastructure); diversion and escape route engineering

---

## Distributed Glitch Processing System (DGPS)

### Local Mode (independent operation)
- **Larson:** Backpack quantum processor — real-time encryption-breaking, exploit generation, active hacking support
- **Law:** Wrist network interface — environmental system hacking for diversions, lockdowns, and escape route manipulation

### Remote Mode
- Firmware hijacking of bio-interfaces
- Cascading network failures across interconnected Mesh systems
- False-signature "ghost presence" generation to evade detection
- Mass data exfiltration

### Resonant Link (within ~50m of each other)
- Combined and multiplicative processing power
- Shared real-time tactical data stream
- Redundancy if one is captured — the other can maintain partial function

### Minor Physical Augmentation (both)
- Reflex boosters (tuned for digital-threat reaction speed, not physical combat)
- Tactical pain suppression
- Metabolic optimization — used to dangerous excess; an active, unaddressed health risk

**Power consumption:** Larson ~15%/min active hacking | Law ~8%/min surveillance and diversion | Combined ~25%/min  
**Recharge:** 2 hours  
**Combat note:** Not effective in direct physical confrontation. Rely entirely on environmental hacking, tactical retreat, and support role for field operatives.

---

## Personality

Obsessive problem-solvers who cannot walk away from unsolved technical challenges — a compulsion that has gotten them captured before and will likely do so again. They understand loyalty, fear, and loss as intellectual concepts but do not experience them as feelings. They treat hacking as play even when the stakes are lethal.

Both privately fear that their consciousness may already be partially distributed into the Mesh's network from so much interfacing — that some non-trivial part of what makes them themselves is now running in a system that doesn't belong to them. Neither has said this to the other. Both suspect the other has the same fear.

---

## Operational Notes

- Their value to the faction is infrastructural — Spirits operations without them would be operationally blind and digitally exposed
- Their greatest liability is the inability to disengage from an interesting problem regardless of tactical context
- The Resonant Link means capturing one without the other is operationally easier than capturing both — but separating them creates an information gap neither half can fully compensate for
