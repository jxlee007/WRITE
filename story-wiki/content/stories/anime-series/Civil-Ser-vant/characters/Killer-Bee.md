# KILLER BEE

**Story:** [Civil Ser-vant](../index.md)
**Type:** Character
**Status:** Active — currently embedded in Mesh infiltration operations
**Role:** Intelligence & Infiltration Specialist — the Spirits
**Real name:** Priya Sharma

---

## Identity

Killer Bee is the Spirits' most effective intelligence operative. She actively infiltrates Mesh facilities on behalf of the faction, reporting directly to Badger. She is loyal to the Spirits — not a mole, not compromised, not a betrayal arc. The conflict she carries is not about where her loyalty lies. It is about whether she still knows who she is after months of being someone else.

> **Continuity note:** Killer Bee's loyalty to the Spirits is confirmed. Do not introduce mole/betrayal ambiguity unless explicitly instructed.

---

## Core Traits

- **Master of emotional flexibility:** Adapts affect, register, and persona to operational context with precision. In infiltration, this is tradecraft. After extended immersion, it is becoming pathology.
- **Weaponized appearance:** Uses beauty and physical presence as a deliberate, calculated infiltration tool. Awareness of how she is perceived is a professional skill, not vanity.
- **Predatory patience:** Holds positions, relationships, and cover for as long as operationally necessary, regardless of personal cost.
- **Designed isolation:** Operates on a need-to-know basis with most faction members. The isolation is self-constructed and professionally maintained.

## Resonance Shield Array (RSA) — Harmonic Wing Configuration

Bio-integrated defensive and mobility system:

| Mode | Capability |
|------|-----------|
| **Standby** | Near-invisible heat-haze shimmer at the body's surface |
| **Full sphere** | ~15-ft protective sphere, reinforced-concrete-equivalent density; survives ballistic, thermal, and concussive attacks; lasts 45–60 sec; charges from absorbed impacts |
| **Wing transformation** | Sphere converts to two geometric wings retaining accumulated charge; offensive energy-discharge strikes (3–5 before depleting); gliding and controlled-descent mobility (not sustained flight or altitude gain) |
| **Stealth veil** | Translucent camouflage + sound dampening; 20–30 min duration; breaks on aggressive action |

**Power consumption:** Sphere ~20%/30 sec | Wing strike ~15%/discharge | Stealth ~8%/min  
**Recharge:** 2–3 hours  
**Note:** The RSA's stealth veil is counterable by Sonar's subsonic resonance system — the sound dampening and force emissions create a detectable interference pattern for sufficiently sensitive acoustic hardware.

## The Actual Arc: Identity Erosion

Killer Bee's internal conflict is not loyalty. It is **identity erosion from extended deep-cover immersion in Mesh systems** — the risk that after months embedded in a Mesh-facing persona, she may lose track of which identity is real: Priya Sharma, Spirits operative, or the cover she has inhabited so thoroughly it has developed its own habitual responses.

Observable signs that Badger has noticed and not addressed:
- Flattening of affect in debriefs (less tonal variation than previously)
- Narrowing emotional range when off-mission
- Increasingly mechanical speech patterns in faction-internal communication

Badger has not pulled her from operations because she is too valuable. This is a choice that has a cost that is accumulating.
