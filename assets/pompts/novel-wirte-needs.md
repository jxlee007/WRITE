## Necessary Prerequisites
Before writing, establish these fundamentals:

* The Core Premise: Write a one-sentence summary of your story.
* Character Profiles: Know your protagonist's main goal and their deepest flaw.
* A Basic Framework: Decide if you will outline everything first or write freely.
* Dedicated Time: Set aside a specific, recurring time each day or week.
* Writing Tools: Choose a reliable software like Scrivener, Google Docs, or Notion. [2, 3, 4, 5, 6] 

## Step-by-Step Guide for Beginners
Follow these steps to transition from ideas to prose execution:
## 1. Start with Low Stakes
Do not try to write a masterpiece on your first attempt. Focus entirely on finishing the first draft. Allow your prose to be messy initially; you cannot fix a blank page. [7, 8, 9, 10] 
## 2. Focus on Scene Mechanics
A novel is built from individual scenes. Every scene needs a clear purpose, a specific setting, and a shift in the character's situation. Practice making your prose move the story forward rather than just describing scenery. [11, 12, 13, 14, 15] 
## 3. Balance Showing and Telling

* Show during high-drama moments by describing physical actions and sensory details.
* Tell during transitions to skip boring parts and speed up the pacing. [16, 17, 18] 

## 4. Establish a Daily Word Goal
Set a small, achievable target. Aiming for 300 to 500 words per day builds consistency without causing creative burnout. [19, 20] 
## 5. Separate Writing from Editing [21] 
Turn off your inner editor while writing the first draft. Write the entire story from beginning to end before you go back to polish sentences, fix grammar, or improve word choices. [22, 23, 24, 25]
